Cover

Wednesday 18 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 17 November morning to Wednesday 18 November morning

Nvidia (lead)
Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of 115.8 billion dollars, more than double a year earlier and well above forecasts, and guided to about 130 billion dollars for the current quarter without any data-centre sales to China. Its board said it will not reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive, and the shares rose 4.8 percent after hours before most of that gain faded overnight.
Asian markets
Asian chip markets steadied on Wednesday after Nvidia's results, with South Korea's Kospi up 1.99 percent to 5,067.44, back above 5,000, while Japan's Nikkei rose 0.28 percent and Taiwan's main index 0.26 percent. Early gains of more than 3 percent in Seoul and about 1.5 percent in Taipei faded during the day.
US markets
Wall Street fell for a fourth straight day on Tuesday ahead of Nvidia's results, with the Dow down 1.64 percent and the S&P 500 down 1.03 percent to its lowest close since late July. October retail sales rose 0.2 percent, as expected, and futures still point to a Federal Reserve rate rise in December.
Energy
Oil rose slightly on Tuesday, with January Brent settling at 93.14 dollars and December West Texas Intermediate at 86.01 dollars, as new US and Iranian strikes around the Gulf offset expectations of a supply surplus. Brent is still down about 20 percent this month as Gulf exports recover.
Ukraine and Russia
Ukrainian drones and missiles brought down the central road section of the Kerch bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, cutting the road link two days before Vladimir Putin meets Donald Trump and Xi Jinping. Moscow says the bridge will be repaired, and Britain and Germany publicly rejected Pentagon criticism of Ukraine's long-range strikes.
APEC and Asia diplomacy
Xi Jinping opened the Asia-Pacific summit in Shenzhen by promising to defend free trade and lower Chinese tariffs, while Putin told the same audience that Western sanctions had failed. Trump arrives from Manila today, and his meetings with Xi and with Xi and Putin together are due on Thursday.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran fired drones at a US radar site in Bahrain on Tuesday and the United States struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, even as Oman and Qatar mediate talks. Kuwait reported drone attacks, and Yemen's Houthis said they targeted a Saudi oil pipeline.
Europe
The European Commission expects the EU economy to grow 1.4 percent this year and next, with inflation of 3.3 percent this year, and warned France and Belgium to bring down deficits above 5 percent of output. It also found, in a preliminary ruling, that the online marketplace Temu breached EU rules on illegal products.
AI
OpenAI released two new models, GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, which an independent benchmarking firm ranks second only to Anthropic's best model and cheaper per task than their predecessors. Microsoft launched seven of its own lower-cost models at its Ignite conference.
AI companies
Anthropic is due to set the price of its share listing after the US close today, with sources saying it may price above the 70 to 78 dollar range, and trading on Nasdaq is due to start on Thursday. Microsoft said its second-generation in-house AI chip, Maia 200, is now running its largest models.
Tesla
Tesla added 200 Cybercab driverless cars to the Texas register in one day, its largest single addition, and unveiled a power-conversion unit called Powercore for grid batteries and data centres. Elon Musk is in China with President Trump's delegation, with no announcement yet on Tesla's driver-assistance software there.
SpaceX
SpaceX delayed its USSF-317 military launch again, to early Friday Paris time, its fifth slip since 9 November. A cargo Dragon capsule is due to leave the International Space Station at 17:00 Paris time today.
India
Cyclone Akvan strengthened into a severe cyclonic storm over the Bay of Bengal and is expected to cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Visakhapatnam on Friday. Forecasters now expect it to stay below the very severe category predicted earlier.
COP31
Negotiators at the UN climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye, say the 18-page draft deal is still a maze of competing proposals on fossil fuels and money for poorer countries. The talks are scheduled to end on Friday.
France
The Senate voted 200 to 129 to restore the 2023 pension reform raising the retirement age to 64, against the government's wishes, and the change is unlikely to survive the National Assembly. Marine Le Pen promised to undo that reform from January 2028 if she wins next year's presidential election.
Morocco
US ambassador to the UN Mike Waltz told Morocco's foreign minister in Rabat that the status quo in Western Sahara is unsustainable and urged the Polisario Front to return to the ceasefire. The two men also discussed the orderly exit of the UN mission there.
Science and technology
NASA's Voyager 1 probe becomes the first human-made object to reach one light day from Earth, about 25.9 billion kilometres, at about 11:16 Paris time today. A large trial found that a light-and-sound headset for Alzheimer's disease did not slow memory loss.
