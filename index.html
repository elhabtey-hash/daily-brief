Cover

Monday 23 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 20 November morning to Monday 23 November morning, including the weekend

Markets (lead)
Wall Street ended its worst week in more than six years on Friday, with the S&P 500 down 3.7 percent and the Nasdaq down 5.3 percent, leaving all three main US indexes at least 10 percent below their records. This morning US futures point to a rebound of 2 to 3 percent, Taiwan rose 2.2 percent after new curbs on short selling, and South Korea fell another 3.3 percent.
AI
An Anthropic mathematician says he proved the Riemann hypothesis, the most famous open problem in mathematics, with the help of the company's Claude model, and the groups behind the Lean proof-checking software say the proof is correct. Mathematicians still have to understand a machine-checked proof that is very long and very hard to read.
Nvidia
Nvidia lost 14 percent on Friday to close at 125.53 dollars, its worst week since 2008, and California's attorney general has served it with an investigative subpoena about chips reaching China and whether investors were misled. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that he still has not received the answers he asked the company for.
AI companies
Anthropic shares fell 8.5 percent on Friday to 88.91 dollars, still above the 83 dollar offer price, as the sell-off spread across AI stocks. Oracle said it has secured all the money it needs for its contract with OpenAI, and Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise report results this week.
Fed
Fed Governor Lisa Cook said on Friday that further rate rises are needed because inflation is not coming down. Futures traders put the chance of a quarter-point rise at the meeting of Tue 8 and Wed 9 Dec at about 91 percent, which would be the second rise since September.
US
Democrats have won at least 258 House seats in this month's midterm elections, the largest majority for any party since 1992, according to AP. President Trump said on the weekend there is a very good chance of deals with Democrats, including on health care.
Energy
Oil rose on Friday, with January Brent settling at 92.57 dollars a barrel, but it still fell 3.4 percent over the week as supply looks ample. January West Texas Intermediate is now the US front month after the December contract expired on Friday at 83.98 dollars.
Iran and the Gulf
Talks to end the US-Iran war remain stalled, with Qatari mediators in Tehran and President Trump saying he wants a permanent deal. Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed strikes on two US air-defence radars in Bahrain, and Saudi jets bombed the Yemeni port of Hodeidah.
COP31
The UN climate summit in Turkey collapsed on Sunday without a deal after the European Union and others rejected a final text that made no mention of fossil fuels. It is the first time since Copenhagen in 2009 that the talks ended with no agreed outcome.
Russia-Ukraine war
A Russian navy corvette, the Merkury, was badly damaged and reported sunk at a naval base in Benghazi, Libya, on Sunday in what Libyan officials called a suspected Ukrainian sea-drone attack. President Zelensky said Russia fired about 2,700 drones and 165 missiles at Ukraine last week.
Europe
EU leaders meet on Thu 26 and Fri 27 Nov to try to agree a new 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine in 2027 and to discuss the next long-term EU budget. Slovakia has already said it will not take part in the loan.
Spain
Spain votes in a snap general election on Sunday 29 Nov, and four polls published this weekend put the conservative People's Party first. The polls suggest the People's Party and the far-right Vox together could win a majority, though one poll has the Socialists only half a point behind.
France
The government is leaning toward forcing the 2027 budget through Parliament without a vote, according to Les Echos. Le Figaro reports that President Macron is also weighing a joint sitting of Parliament in January to write a balanced-budget rule into the constitution.
Mexico
Mexican forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, a Sinaloa drug lord on the FBI's ten most wanted list, in an operation that left at least 11 people dead. The US Justice Department said US intelligence helped.
Sudan
Drone attacks blamed on the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces killed at least four people in and around Khartoum on Sunday and damaged a UN building, according to the UN and local reports.
Science
The European Space Agency said on Saturday that the European-Japanese BepiColombo spacecraft has entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey, only the second mission ever to do so.
Tesla
The two main proxy advisers, ISS and Glass Lewis, recommend voting against re-electing Tesla chair Robyn Denholm at the annual meeting on Thu 3 Dec. Tesla shares fell 7.6 percent on Friday to 345.82 dollars.
SpaceX
SpaceX launched a US spy satellite mission on Sunday just 32 hours after its previous launch from the same California pad, which the company calls a record. Arizona's attorney general has asked Cochise County to delay its 9 Dec vote on a SpaceX-linked data centre that could use up to 6 gigawatts of power.
