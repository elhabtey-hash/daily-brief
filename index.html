Cover

Thursday 24 September 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning

Lead
Morocco's Interior Ministry released preliminary results from Wednesday's legislative election: the Authenticity and Modernity Party (PAM) won 97 of 395 seats, ahead of the National Rally of Independents with 66, Istiqlal with 65, and the Justice and Development Party with 54, on turnout of only 38.02 percent. King Mohammed VI will name the next head of government from the plurality party as coalition talks begin ahead of the 2030 World Cup build-out.
Meta
At Meta Connect overnight, the company confirmed Meta VR Glasses for spring 2027 at 1,299.99 dollars, Ray-Ban Meta Audio camera-free glasses from 349 dollars shipping 13 October, and Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 from 449 dollars available now, with the Muse personal artificial-intelligence agent coming to its glasses. That is the densest consumer hardware and agent keynote of the week and sets the near wearable competition.
Energy
Wednesday oil settled sharply higher — Brent at 103.08 dollars a barrel and West Texas Intermediate at 92.16 — after Iran's hard line at the United Nations and a continued Hormuz stalemate outweighed a United States crude inventory build. Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline low-rate restart and any Yanbu China cargo remain unverified company Claims, while United States diesel still sits near the Energy Information Administration record of 6.529 dollars a gallon.
Iran
Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian told the United Nations General Assembly that Tehran will not bend the knee to United States pressure, a day after President Trump floated annihilating Iran without a deal and claimed envoys had met Iranian officials. Senior Iranian commentary says the sides remain far apart even as shuttle diplomacy continues on the New York sidelines.
China
Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived in Washington for a state visit, receiving a rare personal airport welcome from President Trump, with a White House state dinner due today. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the tariff truce is extended from 10 November to 10 January 2027, buying two more months before the next cliff.
Ukraine
Russian strikes hit Kyiv and other cities as President Volodymyr Zelenskyy addressed the United Nations, with early reports of multiple deaths and dozens injured in the capital area. Zelenskyy urged partners to choke Russia's war revenues and keep air-defence support flowing into winter.
France
The Flamanville-3 European Pressurised Reactor begins its first full outage of about 350 days on Saturday 26 September, reducing French nuclear cover into autumn, and the partial Senate elections follow on Sunday 27 September. The fuel-support package and 2027 budget fight remain the domestic political overlay.
Europe
European Union provisional safeguards on grain-oriented electrical steel and related transformer cores enter force tomorrow Friday 25 September, adding tariff-rate quotas and price floors aimed especially at heavy import dependence. The longer Russia listings renewal agreed Tuesday stays as background, not a new overnight move.
Tesla
Tesla holds the invite-only Semi Rollout today at Sparks, Nevada, inaugurating the dedicated electric heavy-truck factory in a ceremonial event after company-claimed production since about April 2026. Watch for any verified customer deliveries, pricing, or run-rate figures later United States time — none exist yet this Paris morning — plus the separate company Claim that the first Cybercab used in-house Giga Texas cathode.
SpaceX
Starship Flight 14 remains targeted for as early as Monday 28 September with Ship 41 and Booster 21 stacked and a wet dress rehearsal expected today, while Falcon USSF-385 is due Saturday from Vandenberg and Crew-13 stays firm for Thursday 1 October at 11:10 a.m. Eastern. Year-to-date Falcon orbital launches remain 110 with no overnight flight.
Nvidia
Chief Executive Jensen Huang told Ezra Klein's podcast, per Reuters Wednesday, that he opposes blanket antitrust or product-liability waivers for artificial-intelligence firms and prefers product-specific rules, a soft policy Claim with no new company filing. Hard dates ahead are the 1 October cash dividend and the 20–22 October GTC Berlin keynote.
