Cover

Friday 30 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 29 morning to Friday 30 morning

AI spending (lead)
Amazon's cloud arm grew 39 percent and the company plans about 220 billion dollars of spending this year, but its shares fell about 7 percent after hours, a day after Alphabet suffered its biggest one-day fall in years. Investors are rewarding AI demand less and questioning the cost of building it.
Central banks
The European Central Bank and the Bank of Japan both held rates but both signalled that rises may come in December. ECB President Christine Lagarde also announced she will step down on 7 February 2027.
Iran and the Gulf
US Vice President JD Vance has sent Iran a letter, delivered by Qatar, proposing a framework for talks, while US officials warn Gulf allies to prepare for possible strikes after next Tuesday's midterms. Iran has not replied publicly and says it cannot stop Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.
Energy
Oil eased on the hopes for talks, with December Brent settling at 114.88 dollars and December WTI, the US front month, at 102.00 dollars. December Brent expires today, so January Brent, which settled at 110.80 dollars, becomes the headline price.
Israel
The final count leaves Netanyahu's bloc with 53 of 120 seats, the Jewish-led opposition with 48 and Arab-led parties with 19, so no side has a majority. Results go to the president on Wednesday and a hard round of coalition talks lies ahead.
Asia markets
Thursday's closes in yesterday's brief are confirmed, and today Seoul's Kospi fell 3.48 percent and Tokyo's Nikkei fell 2.12 percent at the official close. Chip shares sold off after the US technology results.
Tesla
Tesla's sworn answers to US safety regulators on whether the Cybercab meets federal safety standards are due today, and nothing has been published yet. China's car purchase incentives end tomorrow.
SpaceX
The US Federal Communications Commission unanimously proposed opening much more radio spectrum for satellite-to-phone service, which matters for Starlink, but nothing has been assigned to SpaceX. Both Starship stages for Flight 15 have now been test-fired.
Palantir
Palantir reports third-quarter results on Monday evening, and JPMorgan started coverage with a buy-type rating ahead of the print.
Nvidia
Samsung said its 2027 supply of AI memory chips is sold out, which supports demand for Nvidia's next systems. Amazon and Google both now sell their own AI chips outside their clouds, a slow-burning competitive risk for Nvidia.
AI
Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Carbon, a model for long coding and cybersecurity work that first goes to selected cyber defenders. It tops a widely used independent ranking of model intelligence.
Brazil
Lula has delayed naming his ministers, including the finance minister markets are waiting for, until after a trip abroad, with decisions likely in December.
