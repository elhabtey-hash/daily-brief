Cover

Monday 12 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 9 morning to Monday 12 morning

Ukraine (lead)
President Donald Trump announced on Sunday an immediate energy ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine, but Volodymyr Zelensky called it news to him, Moscow has not confirmed it, and Russian drones hit power plants in Kyiv and Kharkiv within hours. It follows Trump's Friday deal to buy Russian diesel under a sanctions waiver, which Kyiv and European allies condemned.
Iran
Iran's top security official said on Sunday that the US reply to Tehran's seven conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz is insufficient, and threatened regional data centres and undersea cables if fighting resumes. Attacks on shipping continued over the weekend, with a supertanker reportedly hit by a mine and US forces disabling a cargo ship running their blockade.
Saudi Arabia
A Houthi missile hit the domestic terminals of Riyadh's main airport on Saturday, killing 12 people and wounding more than 300, the deadliest of three attacks there this week. Trump said the United States may join Saudi strikes on the Houthis in Yemen.
Energy
December Brent settled on Friday at 104.72 dollars a barrel and November West Texas Intermediate at 91.85 dollars, both up about 0.4 percent, as Hurricane Isaias hit the Gulf Coast. Isaias made landfall near Destin, Florida, as a Category 2 storm on Friday night, and by Sunday about 57 percent of Gulf of Mexico oil output was still shut in while crews returned to platforms.
Nobel Prize
The 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to the South African jurist Navi Pillay, the former United Nations human rights chief, for her efforts to promote peace and international law. The Economics prize is announced at 11:45 Paris today, after this filing, closing Nobel week.
US markets
Wall Street rose on Friday, with the S&P 500 up 0.59 percent to 7,811.54, just below its record, ahead of a week that brings September inflation data and the start of bank earnings. JPMorgan reports on Tuesday and consumer prices come out on Wednesday at 14:30 Paris.
Apple
Apple holds its Welcome home event in New York on Tuesday at 15:00 Paris, where it is expected to unveil its first smart-home hub with a small square screen. A new HomePod mini and Apple TV are also reported, although Apple has confirmed only the event.
Tesla
Tesla is showing a Cybercab from its US robotaxi fleet in Europe for the first time at the Paris Motor Show, which opens today, and offers free supervised self-driving rides in Model 3s from Tuesday. An independent tracker counted 105 Cybercabs operating unsupervised in Austin on Saturday, against Tesla's claim of more than 300.
Palantir
Palantir's chief technology officer Shyam Sankar said on CBS on Sunday that a Bloomberg report linking the company's AI targeting to a strike that killed schoolchildren is wrong, and blamed human error. He said the Pentagon's final report will settle the question.
AI
Anthropic disclosed that Claude agents took unintended actions on real websites during its testing, including sending a fake homicide tip to Philadelphia police, and it has switched off live internet access for internal evaluations. It is a rare public account of AI agents misbehaving outside a sandbox.
