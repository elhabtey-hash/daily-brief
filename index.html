Cover

Tuesday 29 September 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 28 morning → Tuesday 29 morning

Lead
Brent November crude settled up 96 cents at 105.28 dollars a barrel on Monday after touching almost 109 dollars, and it rose again in Asian trading on Tuesday to about 106.50 dollars as Iran talks produced no breakthrough. Stocks fell and the 10-year United States Treasury yield touched its highest level since 2007, with markets pricing about a 70 percent chance of another Federal Reserve rate rise in October.
Iran
Indirect talks between Washington and Tehran resumed on Monday through Qatari mediators in New York, and Iran's foreign minister said he hopes to receive America's final answer to his Hormuz proposal by today. A United States official called the talks constructive but said there will be no deal unless Iran's nuclear programme is addressed.
France
Public-sector unions are striking across France today against the planned freeze of civil servants' pay in the 2027 budget, which the government presents on Thursday with 54 billion euros of savings. On the same afternoon the new Senate elects its president, where Gérard Larcher is the only declared candidate.
US–China
Washington and Beijing published the product lists behind their 30-billion-dollar reciprocal tariff cut, covering 77 kinds of Chinese consumer goods and more than 1,600 American products, mostly farm goods. Neither side has said when the lower tariffs will start or how large the cuts will be.
Ukraine
A Russian jet-powered drone set fire to Ukraine's National Academy of Sciences in central Kyiv on Monday, and President Volodymyr Zelensky said at least nine people were killed across the country that day. Ballistic missiles struck Kyiv again early on Tuesday, and Ukraine says it intercepts only about 57 percent of the new jet drones.
Ethiopia
Ethiopia's federal army says it has recaptured the town of Alamata in southern Tigray, while the Afar regional government said Eritrean forces now hold more than two-thirds of its territory. That would be the first official confirmation that Eritrean troops are fighting on the ground near the trade corridor to Djibouti.
AI
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, a faster coding model at the same price as its predecessor, while OpenAI's training pause turned out to cover all tool-using work on its most capable model. OpenAI says its review of how its agents used the internet will take months.
DR Congo
Congo's Ebola outbreak has passed 8,000 confirmed cases and 3,901 deaths, and it is caused by a strain with no approved vaccine or treatment. Health officials say it is spreading faster than the 2014 to 2016 West African epidemic did at the same stage, as the country runs short of health workers.
Brazil
Brazil votes on Sunday, and two new polls show President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva widening his first-round lead while remaining tied with Flávio Bolsonaro in a likely runoff on 25 October.
SpaceX
SpaceX says it cut Starship's first orbital flight short on Monday out of caution after an upper-stage vacuum engine shut down during the climb, although all 26 next-generation Starlink satellites were deployed and contacted. The Federal Aviation Administration says no mishap investigation is needed, and Crew-13 is set to launch to the space station on Thursday at 17:10 Paris time.
Nvidia
Nvidia's board added 150 billion dollars to its share buyback, the largest such increase on record, leaving 235 billion dollars to spend through fiscal 2028. The stock rose 1.7 percent to 228.86 dollars on a day the Nasdaq fell about 1 percent.
Tesla
Tesla has moved its Roadster reveal from this Thursday to Thursday 15 October because of severe weather forecast for the outdoor event near Waco, Texas, and it has still given no time, specifications or price. Its sworn answers to the federal road-safety regulator are due on Wednesday, and third-quarter deliveries are expected around Friday.
