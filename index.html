Wednesday 28 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 27 morning to Wednesday 28 morning

Israel (lead)
Israel's election produced no clear winner, with all three exit polls putting Netanyahu's bloc and Gadi Eisenkot's opposition bloc at 49 to 52 seats each, short of the 61 needed to govern. A partial official count gives Netanyahu's bloc 56 seats with about 55 percent counted, but around 600,000 absentee and soldier ballots will not be fully counted until Thursday.
AI companies and chips
The AI rally continued, lifting the Nasdaq 1.2 percent, after Oracle said Nvidia's newest Vera Rubin systems are now generally available on its cloud and SK hynix reported record profits with memory-chip demand at twice supply. Seoul's Kospi jumped 6.6 percent today to above 6,000 on the chip news.
Iran and the Gulf
Yemen's Houthis said a missile hit Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and export terminal, and Saudi Arabia said a fire nearby was contained. Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said the war can end only if Iran's conditions are met, while President Pezeshkian met Vladimir Putin in Moscow.
Energy
Oil fell for a second day on the diplomacy, with December Brent settling at 113.88 dollars and December WTI just under 100 dollars at 99.90. December Brent expires on Friday, so the headline Brent price will soon switch to January, which is already near 109 dollars.
Central banks
The Fed announces its decision at 19:00 Paris time tonight, and futures put the chance of a hold at about 90 percent, with a rise in December priced at about 80 percent. The European Central Bank decides on Thursday and the Bank of Japan on Friday.
Big tech earnings
Microsoft and Alphabet report this evening, the first big test of whether AI spending plans hold after last week's sell-off. Amazon follows on Thursday.
Tesla
Texas records now show 573 Tesla Cybercabs authorised as robotaxis, up 110 in a day and more than half of Tesla's 1,080 robotaxis in the state. Tesla's answers to US safety regulators on the Cybercab are due on Friday.
SpaceX
Starlink 15-16 launched on Tuesday morning at 08:25 Paris time, SpaceX's 121st Falcon launch of the year. The US Federal Communications Commission votes on satellite-to-phone spectrum on Thursday.
Brazil
Brazilian markets steadied on Tuesday after Monday's crash, with the Bovespa down 0.5 percent and the real up about 1 percent. Investors are waiting for Lula's choice of economic team.
Ukraine
The local ceasefire in Russian-occupied Oleshky appears to be holding and should last until late Sunday. Russia's foreign minister Sergei Lavrov still said the war is likely to go on and intensify.
