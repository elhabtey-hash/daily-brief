Cover

Wednesday 4 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 3 November morning to Wednesday 4 November morning

US midterms (lead)
The Associated Press (AP) has called both the Senate and the House for the Democrats, so President Trump will face a Congress run by the opposition from January. Democrats flipped at least seven Senate seats, including Texas, Ohio, Kansas and Iowa, but final seat totals are not yet confirmed because counting continues.
Markets
Investors sold AI-linked shares overnight on fears that a Democratic Congress and new Democratic governors in Texas and Ohio will slow the data-centre build-out. Nasdaq futures fell as much as 2 percent before recovering part of the drop, and the dollar and Treasury yields fell.
Asia markets
Tokyo's Nikkei closed down 1.42 percent after being more than 4 percent lower during the session, while Seoul's Kospi recovered to close up 0.62 percent. The bigger falls reported early this morning were intraday moves, not closes.
AI companies
AMD's sales rose 45 percent to 13.4 billion dollars and it forecast about 17 billion for the current quarter, as its newest AI chips now ship in volume to OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft and Anthropic. Arm and Qualcomm report tonight after the US close.
Nvidia
Nvidia closed up 2.29 percent at 206.69 dollars on Tuesday but trades about 2.4 percent lower before the market opens after the election. AMD's strong forecast shows that a real second supplier of AI chips is arriving.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran called the US reply to its plan for reopening the Strait of Hormuz unconvincing, and President Trump confirmed recent US strikes, while Qatar keeps working towards an interim deal. No agreement has been reached.
Energy
January Brent fell 1.1 percent on Tuesday to 103.60 dollars a barrel, its lowest close since 7 October, on hopes for diplomacy. December WTI, the US front month, slipped to 96.09 dollars.
Tesla
Tesla's China-made sales rose 10 percent from a year earlier in October to 67,829 cars but fell 29 percent from September, the lowest month since February. Tesla has asked US safety regulators to keep its Cybercab certification answers confidential.
Palantir
Palantir's quarterly filing confirmed nine-month revenue of 5.9 billion dollars, up 92 percent, and most analysts raised their price targets. The shares closed down 1.65 percent at 175.40 dollars after an early drop as investors weighed slower new contract signings.
SpaceX
SpaceX shares rose 3.62 percent on Tuesday to 141.31 dollars on reports it wants to buy the Boost Mobile network, then fell about 6 percent before the open in the post-election sell-off. Three US military launches are scheduled between Saturday and Friday next week.
France
The National Assembly is still debating the revenue half of the 2027 budget, with 369 amendments left, and a vote could come Wednesday night or Thursday morning. Most observers expect the text to be rejected.
Ukraine
Russia fired about 365 missiles and drones at Ukraine's power grid overnight, and the death toll from the strike on an aid centre in Komyshuvakha has risen to 33, according to President Zelensky. US-mediated talks are still planned in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.
Israel
President Herzog begins consultations with the parties at 17:00 today to decide who gets the first chance to form a government. No bloc has the 61 seats needed for a majority.
