Cover

Wednesday 21 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 20 morning to Wednesday 21 morning

SpaceX and Tesla (lead)
SpaceX swung to a 4.4 billion dollar third-quarter profit on revenue of 14.6 billion dollars, and Elon Musk said on the results call that Tesla and SpaceX boards have each set up committees of independent directors to study a possible merger. Nothing is agreed, and Tesla's own results come tonight at 23:30 Paris time.
Global markets
Wall Street was mixed on Tuesday, with the Dow up 1.02 percent while the Nasdaq slipped 0.02 percent and chip stocks dipped 0.29 percent. Asia bounced on Wednesday after Tuesday's slide, with Seoul's Kospi up about 3 percent in late trade after closing down 3.73 percent the day before.
Iran and Hormuz
Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three tankers hit naval mines while using routes Tehran calls unauthorised, and Foreign Minister Araghchi said the strait stays shut unless Washington accepts Iran's seven-day plan. Traffic is still a tiny fraction of normal.
Energy
Oil touched a five-month high on Tuesday and then fell back, with December Brent settling at 108.79 dollars and December WTI, now the main US contract, at 97.82 dollars. Hopes of US-Iran mediation through Pakistan's army chief outweighed tanker attacks and Houthi strikes.
Nvidia
Reuters reports that Alibaba, ByteDance and Tencent ordered more than 3 million of Nvidia's China-compliant RTX PRO 5500 chips, worth over 35 billion dollars at list price, though Nvidia may supply only a small share. Jensen Huang's GTC Berlin keynote starts at 11:00 Paris time today.
AI companies
Netflix beat profit forecasts but its fourth-quarter outlook disappointed, sending the shares down about 6 percent after hours. Meta reports tonight at 22:30 Paris time, and its spending plans are the next big test for the AI trade.
Ukraine
US envoy Steve Witkoff is due to meet Vladimir Putin after what he called constructive talks with Volodymyr Zelensky in Oslo. Zelensky said US early warning of Russian missile launches has resumed, while Russian strikes overnight killed at least nine people.
Israel and Lebanon
US-hosted military talks between Israel and Lebanon in Tampa resumed on Wednesday without agreement on who oversees southern Lebanon. Lebanon rejects direct military coordination and Israel ties any withdrawal to disarming Hezbollah.
Brazil
Sunday's presidential runoff remains too close to call, with several polls putting Flavio Bolsonaro on 52 percent against 48 percent for President Lula and others showing a tie. The result will shape Brazil's direction on fiscal policy and relations with Washington.
Ebola in East Africa
Kenya has confirmed a ninth Ebola case, all linked to one family cluster, while Congo's outbreak has passed 9,500 confirmed cases. This is the largest Bundibugyo-strain outbreak on record.
