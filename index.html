Cover

Thursday 22 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 21 morning to Thursday 22 morning

Tesla (lead)
Tesla beat profit forecasts with adjusted earnings of 48 cents a share on revenue of 28.05 billion dollars, but its operating margin was only 2.6 percent and the shares fell about 3 percent after hours. Musk said demand exceeds production by a wide margin and that a Cybercab launch in Dallas is planned for next month, which is a company claim.
AI companies
Meta beat revenue forecasts with 64.6 billion dollars and narrowed its 2026 capital spending range upward to 135 to 145 billion dollars. Nasdaq futures still fell about 1.3 percent overnight as investors weighed the Tesla and Meta results.
Global markets
Wall Street rose for a third day on Wednesday, with the Nasdaq up 1.05 percent as SpaceX shares jumped about 7 percent on their results. The Bank of Korea held its key rate at 3 percent this morning after two hikes and signalled more tightening may follow.
SpaceX
Up to 1.31 billion SpaceX shares become eligible for sale today as the first big post-listing lockup ends, although eligibility does not mean they will be sold. The Starlink 15-15 launch was scrubbed again for weather and is now targeted for 00:40 Paris time on Friday.
Iran and Hormuz
Iran told Pakistan's army chief it rejects the latest American demands, while Foreign Minister Araghchi said indirect talks through Pakistan and Qatar continue. Attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz went on, with at least two more vessels reported struck.
Energy
December Brent settled flat at 108.82 dollars and December WTI fell to 97.15 dollars on Wednesday, its first day as the main US contract. A larger than expected 3.1 million barrel fall in US crude stocks was offset by hopes for diplomacy.
Ukraine
Russia agreed in principle to resume three-way talks with the United States and Ukraine, possibly around 10 November in Abu Dhabi, after envoy Steve Witkoff met Vladimir Putin. Russian missiles hit Kyiv hours later, and Zelensky said he could accept a freeze along the current front line.
Nvidia
At GTC Berlin Nvidia announced a large expansion of its supply chain for chip substrates, packaging and testing, and said Google Cloud will offer its Vera Rubin systems next month at a scale of up to 960,000 chips. The conference ends today.
