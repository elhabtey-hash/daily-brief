Cover

Friday 2 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 1 morning to Friday 2 morning

Lead
Brent crude for December delivery, now the front month, settled on Thursday at 102.31 dollars a barrel, up 4.28 dollars, and West Texas Intermediate for November delivery settled at 92.87 dollars, up 2.45. Early Friday in Asia both were little changed from those closes, after Chinese refiners were reported to have halted fuel exports beyond Hong Kong and Macau and after a report that Washington is sending another aircraft carrier toward the Gulf.
Iran
President Trump told reporters on Thursday that Iran will either sign what he called a very fair deal or it will not exist any longer. The Wall Street Journal reported that the United States is sending a third aircraft carrier and as many as 10,000 more troops to the Middle East, while Tehran still has not published the American reply it says it received and the Pentagon has not confirmed the extra deployment.
Ethiopia
Ethiopia said on Thursday that it will close its embassy in Eritrea and ordered ten Eritrean diplomats to leave Addis Ababa within 48 hours, accusing Asmara of hostile acts. Three diplomatic sources told Reuters that explosions were heard in the capital overnight, and a source linked to the rebel alliance said the group had hit military sites, which has not been independently confirmed.
France
The cabinet on Thursday presented the 2027 budget bills, aiming at a deficit of 5 percent of national output, with 43 billion euros of new measures and 54 billion euros once steps already under way are counted. The Senate re-elected Gérard Larcher as its president on the first round with 203 votes, and the National Rally and its allies abstained.
Ukraine
Russia attacked Ukraine overnight with 108 drones, 47 of them jet-powered, and the air force said that by 08:00 in Kyiv it had destroyed or jammed 88, with hits recorded at 11 places. One person was killed when a residential tower in Kyiv was damaged, the South Bridge was struck again, and several drones were still in the air when that count was issued.
SpaceX
NASA said Crew-13 docked to the station at 7:05 in the evening, US Eastern time, on Thursday, the fastest American launch-to-docking in the station's history at 7 hours and 55 minutes, and the hatch opened at 9:19. A Falcon 9 rideshare from California also succeeded and took the year's Falcon count to 113, while a Falcon Heavy flight for the National Reconnaissance Office is still not a confirmed success.
Brazil
A Datafolha poll published on Thursday put President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva at 42 percent and Flávio Bolsonaro at 38 percent for Sunday's first round, with a margin of error of 2 points. The same poll's runoff has Lula at 48 percent and Bolsonaro at 45 percent, so the first round is still open.
Tesla
Texas registered 32 more Tesla Cybercabs, taking that count from 126 to 158, while the Model Y count stayed at 420 and the combined total reached 578. Tesla's third-quarter production and delivery figures were still not out as of about 07:18 Paris time, and the usual United States morning filing window had not started.
US economy
New claims for unemployment insurance fell to 197,000 last week, and the Institute for Supply Management's manufacturing index stayed in expansion at 54.5. The September jobs report is due at 14:30 Paris time today and was not out at filing, with a Dow Jones survey looking for about 84,000 jobs added and unemployment still at 4.1 percent, a forecast rather than a result.
