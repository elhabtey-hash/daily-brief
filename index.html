Cover

Wednesday 25 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 24 November morning to Wednesday 25 November morning

Markets (lead)
Asian stock markets gave back much of Tuesday's surge this morning, with South Korea falling 6.2 percent, Taiwan 4.8 percent and Tokyo 2.8 percent, after US technology shares slipped on Tuesday. Wall Street itself was mixed, with the Dow up 0.9 percent even as the Nasdaq fell 1.5 percent, and US markets close tomorrow for Thanksgiving.
Palantir
New filings show that last week's Palantir share sales were not unplanned selling by executives. Most of the money, about 85 million dollars, was sold automatically to pay tax when restricted stock vested, and director Peter Thiel bought 850,100 shares for about 122 million dollars, more than all those executive sales combined.
Nvidia
The US Commerce Department has added Megaspeed and six affiliates in Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand, the companies at the centre of the alleged chip-diversion network, to its Entity List, a blacklist that in practice bars them from buying US technology. Nvidia shares gave back 5.9 percent of Monday's jump, and Treasury Secretary Bessent's informal request for answers from the board falls today, with no public reply yet.
AI companies
Dell reported a record quarter, with revenue more than doubling to 54.9 billion dollars and its forecast raised again, on soaring demand for servers used in artificial intelligence. Anthropic shares closed below their 83 dollar offer price for the first time, at 81.91 dollars.
Fed
October inflation and growth figures, the last major data before the Fed's December meeting, are due at 14:30 CET today and had not been published when this brief was written. Futures traders put the chance of a quarter-point rise on Wed 9 Dec at 97.4 percent, which would be the second rise since September.
Energy
Oil rebounded on Tuesday, with January Brent settling at 91.40 dollars a barrel, up 2.3 percent, as traders weighed tanker attacks near the Strait of Hormuz against a still ample supply outlook. The January Brent contract remains the headline until it expires on Mon 30 Nov, and January West Texas Intermediate, the US front month, settled at 82.05 dollars.
Morocco
The Court of Arbitration for Sport on Tuesday upheld Senegal's appeal and restored it as the winner of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final, setting aside an earlier African football decision that had awarded the title to Morocco by a 3-0 forfeit. Morocco's football federation said it would pursue all remaining legal avenues.
NATO eastern flank
Poland on Tuesday summoned Russia's ambassador and handed him a strongly worded protest note over the three cruise missiles that entered Polish airspace on Friday. NATO has already agreed to send an early-warning aircraft, and Estonia still plans to close its road border with Russia on Tue 1 Dec.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran's Revolutionary Guards repeated their claim that three oil tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz, including under US escort, a claim a shipping-information centre said was unconfirmed. Qatar, the main mediator, called for freedom of navigation through the strait and a return to talks.
Russia-Ukraine war
A Russian night-time strike on the southern city of Zaporizhzhia killed two people and wounded 48, including four children, according to AP, and Ukrainian officials said nine people were killed across the country in a day. President Zelensky had warned on Monday that Russia was preparing new strikes.
Europe
EU leaders meet in Brussels tomorrow and Friday to try to agree a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine in 2027. Slovakia, Hungary, the Czech Republic and Bulgaria have already said they will not take part.
France
Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu said he will meet parliamentary group leaders next week to keep talking about a 2027 budget deal, without raising the 5 percent deficit target. A joint committee of MPs and senators meets tomorrow on the social security budget, and the government may still have to force the main budget through without a vote.
SpaceX
SpaceX launched a US military mission from California on Tuesday, its 126th Falcon flight of the year, not counting Starship. The shares gave back Monday's gain, and the company is declining about 738 million dollars of US broadband grants, according to an official familiar with the figures.
Tesla
Tesla registered 49 more Cybercab robotaxis in Texas, taking the authorised total to 1,556, and Italy's transport ministry said a supervised version of its self-driving software has begun rolling out to some drivers there. Shareholders vote on chair Robyn Denholm at the annual meeting on Thu 3 Dec, against the advice of the two main proxy firms.
