Cover

Tuesday 17 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 16 November morning to Tuesday 17 November morning

Chips and Asian markets (lead)
Asian stock markets fell for another day on Tuesday as the sell-off in AI chip shares deepened, with Taiwan down 2.31 percent, South Korea's Kospi down 2.59 percent to a nine-month low below 5,000 and Japan's Nikkei down 1.34 percent. The trigger was Monday's slide on Wall Street after the US Commerce Department required export licences for advanced AI chips sold to five more countries, including Singapore and Malaysia.
Nvidia
Nvidia reports third-quarter results tonight at about 22:20 Paris time, the first under interim chief executive Colette Kress, after its shares fell 4.12 percent on Monday to 156.14 dollars. Investors will focus on the outlook for next quarter and on how the company handles the new export rule and pressure from Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent for an outside chief executive.
US markets
Wall Street fell for a third straight day on Monday, with the S&P 500 down 1.92 percent, the Nasdaq down 2.49 percent and the Dow down 1.41 percent. October retail sales come out at 14:30 Paris time, and futures still price an almost certain Federal Reserve rate rise in December.
Energy
Oil fell more than 3 percent on Monday, with January Brent settling at 92.44 dollars and December West Texas Intermediate at 85.72 dollars, as flows through the Strait of Hormuz recover. Iran's Revolutionary Guards say more tankers hit mines in the strait, a claim Reuters could not verify.
APEC and Asia diplomacy
President Trump lands in Manila today for a Southeast Asian summit and a meeting of the Quad group with India, Japan and Australia, before the Asia-Pacific summit in Shenzhen on Wednesday and Thursday. He says he might meet North Korea's Kim Jong Un in China, and Xi Jinping, Trump and Vladimir Putin are due to meet together on Thursday.
Ukraine and Russia
Russian strikes hit two bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv late on Monday, cutting water and power in parts of the city, while Ukrainian drones briefly closed the bridge linking Russia to Crimea. US envoy Jared Kushner says progress is being made towards a deal, though important problems remain.
Europe
EU foreign ministers added about 145 people and companies to sanctions lists on Monday, mostly aimed at Russia's war financing, its oil tanker fleet and its disinformation campaigns. Several ministers said talks with Moscow about Europe's future security would be premature before a ceasefire in Ukraine.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran is warning its people of severe gas shortages this winter because of war damage to processing plants, a sign of strain as US blockades and strikes continue. Peace talks remain stalled, and attacks on Gulf energy sites, including a drone over an Iraqi Kurdish gas field, keep risks high.
AI companies
Anthropic is due to set the price of its stock market listing on Wednesday and start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT, with orders reported at about seven times the shares on offer. Microsoft opens its Ignite conference today, where its in-house AI team has promised new models.
Tesla
Tesla announced a first investment of about 3 billion euros in a new hall at its factory near Berlin to build the Cybercab, its two-seat driverless car, with production planned for 2029. Elon Musk will travel with President Trump to the Asia-Pacific summit in China this week.
SpaceX
SpaceX moved its next military launch, USSF-317, to early Thursday Paris time, and new filings show Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund held SpaceX shares worth about 23 billion dollars at the end of September. Public hearings on the environmental review of its Starbase site in Texas begin tonight.
Palantir
The UK government told Parliament on Monday that Palantir's contract to run the NHS data platform will continue for two more years and end in March 2029, with a new tender starting in 2027. Palantir shares fell 4.52 percent on Monday with the wider tech sell-off.
COP31
The UN climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye, published a first draft deal that calls for an immediate transition away from fossil fuels and for tripling adaptation finance for poorer countries to at least 120 billion dollars. Ministers arrive today for the final stretch, and oil-producing countries are expected to resist the fossil fuel wording.
India
A cyclone named Akvan formed in the Bay of Bengal on Monday evening and India's weather service expects it to become a very severe cyclonic storm before reaching the Andhra Pradesh coast around midday on Friday. Heavy rain is forecast for coastal Andhra Pradesh on Thursday and Friday.
France
The Senate unanimously rejected the government's plan to stop raising most pensions in line with inflation, a key saving in next year's social security budget. Marine Le Pen said France would keep giving Ukraine material and humanitarian aid if she wins next spring's presidential election.
Morocco
The UN peacekeeping mission in Western Sahara, MINURSO, began leaving Laâyoune on Monday after the Security Council failed to renew its mandate at the end of October. Morocco presents the exit as a step toward closing the Western Sahara file at the UN.
Science and technology
Researchers report that a gene variant inherited from Neanderthals seems to make parts of the human brain slightly smaller and may protect against schizophrenia. In China, authorities sacked a scientist whose gene-editing trial ended in the death of a child.
