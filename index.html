Cover

Thursday 1 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 30 morning to Thursday 1 morning

Lead
Brent crude for November delivery, the contract that expired on Wednesday, settled 94 cents higher at 103.53 dollars a barrel, and West Texas Intermediate for November delivery settled 1.04 dollars higher at 90.42. December is the Brent front month from this morning, and a later Asian quote put that December contract at 96.98 dollars, which is a different month and not a collapse from Wednesday's expiry print.
Iran
Iran's government said on Wednesday that Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi had given President Masoud Pezeshkian the official United States reply to Tehran's plan for the Strait of Hormuz, and neither side has published what that reply says. The same day, Britain's maritime warning centre issued three attack notices for tankers struck in the strait on Monday, without naming the ships or who fired.
Iraq
United States Central Command said American forces and their equipment finished leaving Erbil Air Base on Wednesday, which ends the counter-Islamic State mission in Iraq that has run since 2014. The task force is now based in Jordan, and the fight against the Islamic State group in Syria goes on.
US economy
The Federal Reserve's preferred price gauge rose 3.4 percent in the year to August, and 3.0 percent once food and energy are left out, both lighter than forecast, while the final reading of spring growth was revised up to 2.2 percent. Stocks were mixed on Wednesday, the 10-year Treasury yield finished near 5.30 percent, and the chance of another rate rise in October fell to about 37 percent.
Ethiopia
A hospital medic told the BBC that at least 52 civilians were killed in three days of fighting around Alamata in Tigray, and Médecins Sans Frontières said more than 700 wounded people had reached hospitals it supports. Tourists are still stuck in Lalibela, phones and banking in Tigray are cut, and the two sides still disagree on who started the shooting.
Ukraine
Russia attacked Ukraine overnight with 107 drones, 63 of them jet-powered, and Ukraine's air force said that by 08:00 it had destroyed or jammed 87 of them, with hits recorded at 11 places. Several drones were still in the air when that count was issued, so the figure is not final.
France
Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu's 2027 budget, built around 54 billion euros of savings and a deficit target of 5 percent of national output, is on the cabinet agenda today. The Senate elects its president at 15:00 this afternoon, and Gérard Larcher, in the post since 2014, is the only declared candidate.
SpaceX
NASA and SpaceX are still aiming to launch Crew-13 to the space station today at 17:10 Paris time, after a launch readiness review gave a go and with the weather odds now at 55 percent favorable, down from 60 percent. The rocket had not lifted off and no scrub had been posted as of about 07:15 Paris, and the backup is Friday at 16:47 Paris time.
Morocco
Fatima Zahra El Mansouri held a first round of coalition talks on Wednesday and no party has signed on, leaving her Authenticity and Modernity Party on 97 of 395 seats, short of the 198 needed for a majority. The talks resume at party headquarters today, including a meeting with the Islamist party that still says it will stay in opposition.
AI companies
The United States Federal Trade Commission has opened an investigation into OpenAI, Anthropic and other artificial-intelligence companies over the dangers their products may pose, a spokesperson confirmed on Wednesday. The spokesperson would not name the other firms, and neither OpenAI nor Anthropic had answered when that was reported.
