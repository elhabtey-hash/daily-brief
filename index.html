Cover

Thursday 12 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 11 November morning to Thursday 12 November morning

Nvidia (lead)
Several major outlets report that Nvidia’s board met late on Wednesday to decide whether to remove chief executive Jensen Huang, with some directors said to favour finance chief Colette Kress as interim leader. Nvidia has made no statement and no decision has been announced, and the shares fell about 8 percent in after-hours trading.
US markets
Wall Street rebounded on Wednesday, with the S&P 500 up 1.31 percent and the Nasdaq up 1.60 percent as chip stocks rallied on CoreWeave’s results. Futures for interest rates still price a high chance of a second Federal Reserve rate rise in December.
Japan, Korea and Taiwan
Asian markets fell on Thursday on the uncertainty at Nvidia, with Taiwan down 2.63 percent, the Nikkei down 0.70 percent and the Kospi down 0.61 percent. Taiwan was also weighed down by a speech in which China’s President Xi Jinping again called unification with the island an unswerving task.
Ukraine
Two days of US-brokered talks with Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi ended with agreement in principle on a large prisoner exchange but no breakthrough on ending the war. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are due in Moscow today to meet President Putin, then in Dublin to brief President Zelensky.
Iran and the Gulf
The Wall Street Journal reports that Vice President JD Vance is weighing direct talks with Iran’s top security official, who says no negotiations are taking place. Iran repeats that the Strait of Hormuz stays shut until the US accepts its seven conditions.
Energy
January Brent settled up 1.39 percent at 92.76 dollars a barrel on Wednesday, recovering from a two-month low as hopes for a quick end to the Iran war faded. December West Texas Intermediate settled at 86.53 dollars.
Europe
Around 50 European leaders meet in Dublin today for the European Political Community, and EU leaders hold their first summit discussion devoted to artificial intelligence there tomorrow. Zelensky is expected to attend, and the Abu Dhabi outcome will hang over both meetings.
United Kingdom
The UK economy grew 0.7 percent in the third quarter, its fastest pace in two years and in line with forecasts. Growth came almost entirely from services.
AI companies
Anthropic’s share sale roadshow is drawing heavy demand according to Dow Jones, while Foxconn, which assembles Nvidia’s AI servers, reported a 34 percent jump in quarterly profit. Anthropic’s latest filing also shows it has committed up to 120.5 billion dollars for computing power from SpaceX’s AI arm.
AI
Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, which predicts how changes in DNA affect individual cell types, and a new version of its robot-control model, Gemini Robotics 2.5. Meta released Muse Ember, a small, cheap model built for routine tasks run by AI agents.
SpaceX
A Falcon 9 rocket launched a classified US military satellite at 05:36 Paris time today, SpaceX’s 123rd Falcon launch this year. Starship Flight 15 is targeted for Friday night but still awaits regulatory approval.
Tesla
Tesla says its robotaxi service now runs around the clock in every city where it operates, which has not yet been independently checked. Elon Musk said Cybertruck production will continue until the end of summer 2027, and Finland approved Tesla’s assisted-driving system.
Morocco
Storm Charles brought heavy rain, hail and winds of up to 120 kilometres an hour to Morocco, and schools were closed in 12 provinces. The storm is also hitting Spain and Portugal.
Science and technology
An independent team reanalysed the data behind the first image of a black hole and found the same ring shape, answering a long-running challenge to the 2019 result. The study was published in Nature on Wednesday.
