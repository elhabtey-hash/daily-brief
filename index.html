Cover

Monday 16 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 13 November morning to Monday 16 November morning, weekend included

Taiwan, Korea and Japan (lead)
Asian chip stocks slumped on Monday as worries about the AI boom after Jensen Huang’s exit from Nvidia spread, with Taiwan’s main index down 6.03 percent, the Kospi down 3.59 percent and the Nikkei down 2.42 percent. Japan’s economy also shrank slightly in July to September, against forecasts of modest growth, as higher energy costs hit spending.
Nvidia
Nvidia reports third-quarter results on Tuesday at about 22:20 Paris time, its first under interim chief executive Colette Kress, with analysts expecting revenue of about 109 billion dollars. Over the weekend two large investors and an online petition urged the board to leave room for Huang’s return, which the company has not addressed.
US markets
Wall Street ended Friday mixed but posted its best week since August, with the S&P 500 down 0.40 percent on the day and up 2.5 percent on the week. Wholesale prices rose 0.3 percent in October, a little below forecasts, and futures still price a second Federal Reserve rate rise in December.
US air travel
A failure in air traffic control communications forced a nationwide ground stop on Friday, the longest since the 2001 attacks, and thousands of flights were cancelled over the weekend. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy expects normal operations by Monday.
Energy
Oil was little changed on Friday, with January Brent settling at 95.84 dollars and December West Texas Intermediate at 88.56 dollars, both down about 2.5 percent on the week. The International Energy Agency said the market has swung into surplus for the first time since the Iran war began.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran launched drones at a US radar site in Bahrain on Friday and again on Sunday, the first such attacks since September, and its Revolutionary Guards say a supertanker sank after hitting a mine in the Strait of Hormuz. The tanker claim has not been independently confirmed, and peace talks remain stalled.
Ukraine and Russia
President Zelensky says US negotiators asked whether Ukraine would give up the Donbas region in exchange for joining NATO, the first time the two ideas have been linked in talks. US envoy Jared Kushner says he is working to bring Europe and Russia into direct talks, while both sides kept up heavy drone strikes.
Europe
After the Dublin summit, Commission President von der Leyen promised a proposal to fund Europe’s technology independence and a 22nd package of sanctions on Russia. The EU and Britain hold a summit in Brussels on Friday, where London hopes to agree closer ties on food trade and youth mobility.
China
China’s economy slowed further in October, with retail sales up only 0.4 percent on a year earlier and factory output up 4.8 percent, both below forecasts. Xi Jinping hosts the Asia-Pacific summit in Shenzhen on Wednesday and Thursday, including the first three-way meeting with Presidents Trump and Putin.
AI companies
Anthropic plans to set the price of its stock market listing on Wednesday and begin trading on Nasdaq on Thursday, with orders reported at about six times the shares on offer. Microsoft opens its Ignite conference on Tuesday, where it says its own AI team will show new models.
SpaceX
Starship Flight 15 launched on Friday night and the ship was caught by the launch tower on Saturday evening, the first time SpaceX has caught a Starship upper stage. The Super Heavy booster was lost over the Gulf of Mexico after an automatic abort, and the aviation regulator has not yet said whether it will require an investigation.
Tesla
Tesla began inviting selected customers to order the Cybercab for personal use at 29,990 dollars plus a required 299-dollar monthly subscription, and Elon Musk then said the fee should be closer to 115 dollars. Today is the first trading session since, and no price change has been announced.
Science and technology
A Vulcan rocket from United Launch Alliance failed about 10 minutes after launch on Sunday, destroying 40 satellites for Amazon’s Leo internet network. It is the company’s first mission failure, and the cause is not yet known.
COP31
The UN climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye, enter their second week split over whether to keep the 1.5 degree warming limit as the benchmark after the UN said it will be exceeded. Hosts Türkiye and Australia are pushing a deal on electrification, which critics say does too little on fossil fuels.
Congo
Africa’s public health agency says the Ebola outbreak in Congo is still not under control six months after it was declared, with more than 11,000 confirmed cases and about 5,500 deaths. The World Health Organization called for a halt to fighting that is hampering the response.
Sudan
Sudan’s army says it has taken three areas in West Kordofan and is advancing on the paramilitary-held town of Al-Nuhud, according to military sources. A monitoring group says drone strikes in the state killed at least 15 civilians.
India
India’s weather service expects a depression in the Bay of Bengal to become a severe cyclonic storm by Tuesday and head toward the Andhra Pradesh coast later in the week. It would be the third cyclone in the region in three weeks.
France
The 2027 budget, rejected by the National Assembly, is now in the Senate, and the government is weighing whether to force it through or use decrees after 9 December. French media report that a cyberattack on a La Poste subsidiary exposed data on about 22 million people.
Morocco
Morocco’s lower house passed the 2027 budget bill on Saturday by 179 votes to 126, the first budget of Prime Minister El Mansouri’s government. The bill now goes to the upper house.
Cape Verde
Provisional results show President José Maria Neves re-elected in Sunday’s first round with 63.8 percent of the vote. Final official results are still to come.
Czech Republic
Hundreds of thousands of people rallied in Prague on Sunday against a government plan to scrap the public media licence fee and fund broadcasters from the state budget. Organisers called it possibly the largest protest since 1989, a figure not independently confirmed.
