Cover

Tuesday 27 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 26 morning to Tuesday 27 morning

AI companies and chips (lead)
Anthropic filed to go public on Monday, showing 16.3 billion dollars of revenue in the first half of 2026, and the filing revived the AI trade, lifting the chip index 2.8 percent. Meta priced a 37.5 billion dollar share sale to fund AI spending, with Nvidia putting in 3 billion dollars as Meta expands its use of Nvidia systems.
Brazil
Brazilian stocks fell about 14 percent on Monday after Lula's re-election, while Flavio Bolsonaro dropped his call to annul the vote and his party accepted the result. Donald Trump said the election looked rigged, without evidence.
Iran and the Gulf
Saudi, Pakistani and Turkish leaders met Iran's president in Tehran and agreed to keep talking, while Saudi air defences shot down more Houthi missiles and drones overnight. Iran's military chief says the Strait of Hormuz is completely closed to ships that do not follow Iranian rules.
Egypt
Egypt's parliament authorised President Sisi to give Saudi Arabia military support against the Houthis, by the required two-thirds majority in a closed vote. Cairo says the mission is defensive only and will not include attacks on Yemen.
Energy
Oil eased on Monday as Gulf talks began, with December Brent settling at 115.81 dollars and December WTI at 101.76 dollars. December Brent expires on Friday, so the headline Brent price will switch to the January contract.
Central banks
The Fed starts its two-day meeting today and markets put the chance of a hold on Wednesday at about 90 percent, with a December rise widely priced. The European Central Bank on Thursday and the Bank of Japan on Friday are both expected to hold.
Israel
Israelis are voting today in an election seen as a referendum on Prime Minister Netanyahu, with polls showing neither bloc reaching a majority. Polls close at 21:00 Paris time and no result is known at filing.
SpaceX
A company filing shows SpaceX's 40 billion dollar chip deal is a binding commitment for an Apollo-led lease of Nvidia Vera Rubin systems that has not yet closed, contrary to Elon Musk's claim that it had. Starlink 15-16 is due to launch at 09:25 Paris time this morning and had not flown at filing.
Tesla
Latvia approved Tesla's supervised Full Self-Driving software, the tenth EU country to do so, and Barclays upgraded the shares. Answers to US safety regulators on the Cybercab robotaxi are due Friday.
Asia markets
Seoul jumped about 3 percent and Tokyo about 1.7 percent today on the AI rebound and cheaper oil. Monday's falls in both markets are confirmed by official closes.
Ukraine
A US-brokered local ceasefire began in Russian-occupied Oleshky today to let aid in and civilians out, hours after Russian missiles hit Kyiv. It is small but the first practical deal ahead of proposed three-way talks in November.
