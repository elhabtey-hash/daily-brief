Cover

Monday 5 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 2 morning to Monday 5 morning (whole weekend)

Lead
Flávio Bolsonaro won Sunday's first round of Brazil's presidential election with about 47 percent of the valid vote to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's about 45 percent, short of a majority, so they meet again in a runoff on Sunday 25 October. Abstention was about 21 percent, a first-round record in the modern series.
Energy
Brent crude for December delivery settled on Friday at 102.25 dollars a barrel, down 6 cents from Thursday, and West Texas Intermediate for November delivery settled at 91.11 dollars, down 1.76 dollars. Early Monday in Asia both were lower again after the Group of Seven agreed to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude and after OPEC Plus left November quotas unchanged.
US economy
The United States added only 29,000 jobs in September and unemployment rose to 4.2 percent, the Labor Department said on Friday, with August revised down to 133,000. Stocks closed higher and market odds of another Federal Reserve rate rise this month fell sharply after the soft print.
Tesla
Tesla said on Friday that it produced 464,391 vehicles and delivered 486,532 in the third quarter, with 13.7 gigawatt-hours of energy storage deployed. Deliveries were about 2 percent below the same quarter last year and a little above the second quarter, and the company set earnings for Wednesday 21 October after the United States close.
Iran
Iranian officials said over the weekend that they are reviewing an American reply to their Hormuz proposal but have not published the text, and the parliament speaker said the strait will stay shut until seven conditions from a June memorandum are met. Reports of a third United States aircraft carrier and thousands more troops headed to the region remain newspaper accounts without a Pentagon confirmation found this morning.
Ukraine
Russia attacked Ukraine overnight into Monday with 205 drones, 49 of them jet-powered, and the air force said that by 08:00 in Kyiv it had destroyed or jammed 172, with hits at 13 places. Kyiv's Northern Bridge was struck again on Sunday in a multi-day campaign against the capital's river crossings.
SpaceX
Falcon Heavy NROL-97 is now a confirmed success from Friday's attempt, taking the Falcon year count to 114, with no further Falcon flight over the weekend. A Space Development Agency Falcon 9 from Vandenberg is targeted for 10:17 Paris today with a backup at the same clock time on Tuesday, and it had not flown at filing.
Nobel
The Nobel Prize week opens today with Physiology or Medicine due at 11:30 Paris, then Physics on Tuesday, Chemistry on Wednesday, Literature on Thursday, Peace on Friday, and Economics on Monday 12 October. No prize had been announced at filing.
