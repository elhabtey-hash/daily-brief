Cover

Tuesday 10 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 9 November morning to Tuesday 10 November morning

US markets (lead)
Wall Street had its best day since April on Monday, with the S&P 500 up 2.35 percent and the Nasdaq up 2.74 percent, as oil fell sharply and chip stocks rebounded. October consumer prices, due at 14:30 Paris time today, are the next test of that rally and of a likely December rate rise.
Iran and the Gulf
President Trump said he will hold off on new strikes on Iran for now after appeals from Qatar and other Gulf states, but will keep the naval blockade around the Strait of Hormuz. Attacks on tankers continue and US and Gulf officials doubt a deal is close.
Energy
January Brent settled down 5.85 percent at 92.57 dollars a barrel on Monday and December West Texas Intermediate fell 4.38 percent to 86.85 dollars. Prices kept falling after the settlement, extending last week’s steepest weekly drop since 2020.
Japan, Korea and Taiwan
Asian chip markets rallied again today, with the Kospi up 4.39 percent, the Nikkei up 2.87 percent and back above 60,000, and Taiwan up 2.52 percent. Taiwan’s regulator has cut the limit on short selling to calm the market.
China
China’s exports rose 35.2 percent in October from a year earlier and imports jumped 41.6 percent, the fastest rise since 2021. The trade surplus for the year so far has passed one trillion dollars a week before President Xi hosts President Trump at the Asia-Pacific summit.
Ukraine
US, Russian and Ukrainian negotiators begin two days of talks in Abu Dhabi today, the first substantive round since February. Washington is also pitching a new security arrangement between Russia and Europe, which worries European governments.
France
France formally blamed Iran’s Revolutionary Guards for October’s cyberattack on supermarket refrigerators at more than 700 sites and promised a response, including a push for EU sanctions. The Senate is now debating the social security budget after the National Assembly rejected it.
Nvidia
Nvidia shares fell 4.27 percent on Monday while the wider chip index rose 6.85 percent, as investors treated the board inquiry about Jensen Huang as a company-specific risk. Nvidia must answer the House request for a closed-door briefing by Wednesday.
AI companies
TSMC’s October revenue rose 59.7 percent from a year earlier, a sign that demand for AI chips is still strong. CoreWeave reports results tonight with its debt under strain, and Anthropic is reported to be starting investor meetings for a stock market listing.
Tesla
Tesla said its robotaxi service would open in San Antonio, its eighth city, but the start date is unclear and no rides have been verified yet. Tesla sold only 8,817 cars in China in October as exports made up a record 87 percent of Shanghai output.
SpaceX
A city notice shows SpaceX plans a gas-fired power plant and supercomputing site at Starbase costing about 24 billion dollars, with a tax-break hearing on 10 December. Its next military Falcon 9 launch slipped to early Wednesday, and Starship Flight 15 still awaits regulatory approval.
COP31 climate talks
The UN climate summit opened in Antalya, Türkiye, on Monday and runs to 20 November, with Australia leading the negotiations. Organisers want practical decisions on finance, clean energy and adaptation rather than new targets.
Europe
The EU’s foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas pushed back at the G7 on the idea of a new Russia-Europe security architecture, and the EU joins European delegations in Abu Dhabi. The Commission also pledged 50 million euros for a Black Sea maritime security hub in Romania and Bulgaria.
Palantir
The White House nominated Palantir’s chief information security officer, Michael LoSapio, to run the US cybersecurity agency. The Guardian reports Palantir lobbied a UK minister to bring family-doctor records into its NHS software, which Palantir says reflects a long-held view.
AI
Google released Gemini 4 Argon, a model designed to reason about the physical world and help control robots. Google says it leads other frontier models on one robotics reasoning test, a company claim that has not been independently checked.
Morocco
A Moroccan health workers’ union called a national strike for Tuesday over government commitments and budget cuts. It comes a week after the UN peacekeeping mission in Western Sahara ended, with Morocco pledging to keep the ceasefire.
Capgemini
Capgemini won an expanded AI-led transformation contract with Telia Sweden, a client of more than 40 years. No contract value was disclosed.
