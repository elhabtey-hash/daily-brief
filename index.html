Cover

Thursday 26 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 25 November morning to Thursday 26 November morning

Fed (lead)
The US government's preferred inflation gauge rose 0.3 percent in October and 3.8 percent on the year, a touch faster than in September, and a revised estimate showed the economy grew at an annual rate of 4.3 percent in the third quarter, much faster than first thought. Futures traders now put the chance of a quarter-point rate rise on Wed 9 Dec at 99.8 percent, which would be the second rise since September.
Markets
US stocks finished mixed on Wednesday after recovering from a sharp early drop in technology shares, and markets are closed today for Thanksgiving, with a short session on Friday. In Asia this morning, Taiwan rose 2.5 percent, Tokyo 0.9 percent and South Korea 0.3 percent, after South Korea's central bank raised its key rate to 3.25 percent.
Energy
Oil jumped on Wednesday, with January Brent settling at 94.61 dollars a barrel, up 3.5 percent, after Yemen's Houthi movement said it had attacked Saudi Arabia's largest oil refinery, a claim Riyadh had not confirmed. January West Texas Intermediate, the US front month, settled at 83.68 dollars, and US oil trading is closed today.
Nvidia
Treasury Secretary Bessent's informal request for answers from Nvidia's board passed with no public statement from the company or from the Treasury. The official Congress calendar now lists a Senate Banking hearing on export controls for Thu 3 Dec, though Nvidia chief Jensen Huang's appearance as a witness has still not been officially confirmed, and shares fell 3.9 percent on Wednesday.
SpaceX
The US communications regulator authorised SpaceX to deploy a constellation of up to one million satellites for orbital data centres, in an order issued on Wednesday under delegated staff authority rather than a full commission vote. The company also said it would gift one newly issued share to every currently active Trump Account, with no share count or dollar value given, and only if approvals are granted.
Tesla
The European Commission has moved a committee meeting on Tesla's assisted-driving software from 1 December to 15 December, according to a Commission spokesperson, though a vote has still not been confirmed and the committee has never voted on the files. Tesla also said its paid robotaxi service in Dallas had just grown wider.
Europe
European Union leaders meet in Brussels today and tomorrow to try to narrow differences on a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine in 2027 and on the bloc's next long-term budget. Hungary, Slovakia, the Czech Republic and Malta still want to be excused from the Ukraine loan.
NATO eastern flank
Poland said it plans a no-fly zone along its eastern border with Ukraine, and asked NATO allies to consider intercepting Russian missiles over Ukrainian territory if they are heading towards the alliance. A Polish deputy defence minister said NATO had rejected that idea because it would create numerous complications.
Russia-Ukraine war
A Russian attack on Ukraine's energy system damaged power and gas sites and cut electricity in five regions, Ukrainian officials said, and later on Wednesday ballistic missiles struck a shopping centre and other buildings in Kyiv. Local authorities said at least five people were killed in strikes across the country.
Iran and the Gulf
Yemen's Houthi movement said it had attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery, the kingdom's largest, a claim the Saudi government and state oil company had not confirmed. Qatar continues to press for freedom of navigation through the Strait of Hormuz and a return to talks.
Morocco
Morocco's football federation is preparing to take the Africa Cup of Nations ruling to the Swiss Federal Tribunal, the only court that can review awards from the Court of Arbitration for Sport, and has 30 days once the reasoned award is notified. That court rarely sets aside such awards, and no formal filing had been confirmed when this brief was written.
France
Fourteen deputies and senators meet today in a joint committee to try to agree a common social-security budget for 2027, after the Senate passed its version and the National Assembly earlier rejected the government's text. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu said he had no wish to force the issue, while leaving open the use of article 49.3, which lets the government adopt a bill without a full vote.
AI companies
Dell shares rose 0.6 percent in regular Wednesday trading, to 297.06 dollars, after Tuesday's record results, a modest follow-through after a sharp drop on the cash close the day of the report. Anthropic shares closed at 82.79 dollars, still just under their 83 dollar offer price.
