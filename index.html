Tuesday 6 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 5 morning to Tuesday 6 morning

Yemen (lead)
Saudi-led coalition warplanes backed a Yemeni government offensive on Monday to retake the Bab el-Mandeb Strait at the mouth of the Red Sea from the Houthis, and Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkey agreed to send forces to the kingdom under their defence pact. The Houthis said they hit Riyadh's main airport and an Aramco refinery at Rabigh, which Saudi Arabia has not confirmed.
Nobel Prize
The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine went on Monday to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for discovering light-gated ion channels and founding optogenetics, the method of switching chosen brain cells on and off with light. The Physics prize is due at 11:45 Paris today, after this filing, with Chemistry on Wednesday, Literature on Thursday, Peace on Friday and Economics on Monday 12 October.
Energy
Brent crude for December delivery settled on Monday at 100.32 dollars a barrel, down 1.93 dollars, and West Texas Intermediate for November delivery settled at 89.43 dollars, down 1.68 dollars, as Gulf exports recovered and the Group of Seven stock release sank in. Early Tuesday in Asia both were roughly flat, with December Brent near 100.60 dollars despite the Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia.
Brazil
Brazilian stocks jumped 7.7 percent on Monday to a record close above 200,000 points and the real gained about 4 percent to 5.00 per dollar after Flávio Bolsonaro's first-round lead, while his Liberal Party won 121 lower-house seats and is set to hold 28 of the 81 Senate seats. Two defeated right-wing candidates, Ronaldo Caiado and Romeu Zema, endorsed him for the runoff against President Lula on Sunday 25 October.
Ethiopia
Federal forces and allied fighters have taken Mekelle, the capital of Tigray, after the Tigray People's Liberation Front said it had pulled out to fight on from elsewhere, reviving the war that a 2022 peace deal ended. Ethiopia now accuses Eritrea of fighting alongside the Tigrayan forces, which Eritrea denies, days after the two countries cut diplomatic ties.
Spain
Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez called a snap general election for Sunday 29 November after parliament rejected his housing measures amid large protests over rents. Polls point to a People's Party government relying on the far-right Vox, which would bring the far right into national government for the first time since the Franco era.
France
The National Rally formed its first-ever group in the French Senate on Monday, with 15 members including its allies, as the official group lists were published. French ten-year borrowing costs eased to about 4.86 percent after touching 4.99 percent on Friday, but the euro still slid to a 17-month low near 1.116 dollars.
Ukraine
Russia launched 137 drones at Ukraine overnight into Tuesday, 49 of them jet-powered, and the air force said 114 were downed or jammed by 08:00 Kyiv time with hits at 17 places. Kyiv has accepted an American proposal for technical-level talks with Russia by the end of October, and the Kremlin said talks this month cannot be ruled out.
US markets
The Nasdaq closed at a record on Monday and the S&P 500 rose 0.7 percent, led by Nvidia and other artificial intelligence stocks, even as the ten-year Treasury yield held near 5.31 percent, its highest in a year. Minutes of the Federal Reserve's September meeting are due on Wednesday, and the September consumer price report follows on Wednesday 14 October.
