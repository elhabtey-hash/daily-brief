Cover

Wednesday 11 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 10 November morning to Wednesday 11 November morning

US inflation (lead)
US consumer prices rose 0.4 percent in October, more than the 0.3 percent expected, and core prices rose 0.3 percent for a third straight month. That makes a December interest rate rise by the Federal Reserve close to certain in market pricing, and some officials now openly discuss a half-point move.
US markets
Wall Street gave back part of Monday’s rally, with the S&P 500 down 0.38 percent and the Nasdaq down 0.74 percent, as bond yields jumped and Nvidia fell another 5.5 percent. Futures pointed higher overnight after CoreWeave’s strong results.
Japan, Korea and Taiwan
Asian chip markets rose for a third day, with Taiwan up 3.62 percent as TSMC closed at a record, the Kospi up 2.60 percent and the Nikkei up 1.31 percent. Investors looked past the US inflation scare and took CoreWeave’s results as a sign that AI demand is holding up.
AI companies
CoreWeave, which rents out Nvidia chips, beat forecasts with 3.66 billion dollars of quarterly revenue, raised its outlook and said it will not borrow on unsecured terms at today’s high yields. Anthropic launched the roadshow for its share sale at 70 to 78 dollars a share, which would value it at up to about 1.4 trillion dollars on a fully diluted basis.
Ukraine
The first US-Russia-Ukraine talks since February ended their first day in Abu Dhabi with Kyiv calling them constructive and the US envoys saying gaps had narrowed. A second day today covers prisoner exchanges, energy and the Black Sea, while Russia’s demand for all of Donetsk remains the main obstacle.
Iran and the Gulf
Qatar says its mediation between Washington and Tehran is gaining momentum, but a senior Iranian official says no negotiations are taking place. Reuters reports that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has moved to a military base after Iranian threats against him.
Energy
January Brent settled down 1.17 percent at 91.49 dollars a barrel on Tuesday, its lowest close since 31 August, after dipping below 90 dollars during the session. December West Texas Intermediate settled at 86.04 dollars.
Nvidia
Nvidia’s independent directors hired Wachtell Lipton and former acting federal prosecutor Joon Kim to run the investigation into Jensen Huang, and the shares fell 5.47 percent. Today is the deadline for Huang to answer a House request for a closed-door briefing on 18 November.
Israel
Israel’s new parliament was sworn in without a government, and former army chief Gadi Eisenkot has until 6 December to build a majority. His bloc immediately tabled bills for an inquiry into the 7 October 2023 attacks and term limits for prime ministers.
US politics
The House voted 211 to 204 to send an impeachment resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, which effectively ends it. House Republicans chose Jim Jordan to lead them in the minority in the next Congress.
China and the US
Treasury Secretary Bessent said next week’s Trump-Xi meeting in Shenzhen will start a dialogue on the risks of advanced AI, including how each side tests models before release. It will be the leaders’ third meeting this year.
COP31 climate talks
World leaders begin speeches at the UN climate summit in Antalya today, including European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and Brazil’s President Lula. Host Türkiye wants a target to raise electricity’s share of world energy use to 35 percent by 2035.
Europe
EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said sanctions on Russia will be kept and tightened despite the peace talks, and the Commission is setting up a dedicated EU sanctions office. The EU’s auditors found the trade sanctions are working.
AI
Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, its most capable model, aimed at long coding projects, cybersecurity and scientific research. Google says it tops several outside benchmarks at lower cost, which has not yet been checked independently.
Tesla
Tesla’s robotaxi service in San Antonio is confirmed running, with a local reporter taking a paid 16 dollar ride in a car with no safety driver. A Semi truck service centre is due to open in Laredo, Texas, on Thursday, according to an enthusiast site.
SpaceX
US regulators opened public review of SpaceX’s purchase of 800 megahertz airwaves from Grain, and a German supplier will build SpaceX a solar-cell production line for its planned computing satellites. The next military Falcon 9 launch slipped again to early Thursday, and Starship Flight 15 still lacks regulatory approval.
Palantir
Palantir’s chief technology officer Shyam Sankar filed notice of a planned sale of 300,000 shares worth about 51 million dollars. A UK investigative outlet reports that former Metropolitan Police chief Cressida Dick has been a paid adviser to the company since 2025.
France
The Senate continues work on the 2027 social security budget, with a vote due on 18 November before the state budget arrives on 20 November. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu still has no majority to pass either.
Science and technology
Caltech and Northwestern researchers used a digital twin of the James Webb Space Telescope to strip away starlight and found a planet that had been hidden in existing images. The method makes faint planets two to 15 times easier to detect.
Morocco
Gulf states backed Morocco’s autonomy plan for Western Sahara in a UN debate, while exporters warned that a ban on vegetable exports is costing them African markets. Israeli defence officials visited Rabat to deepen military ties.
Capgemini
Capgemini and the Dutch research institute TNO launched DebtZero, a service that uses automated tools to clean up old, error-prone software in large companies. No financial terms were disclosed.
