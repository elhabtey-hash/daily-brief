Cover

Monday 19 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 16 morning to Monday 19 morning

Global markets (lead)
Wall Street fell on Friday as the sell-off in chip and AI stocks deepened, with the Nasdaq Composite down 2.14 percent to 26,140.03, the S&P 500 down 1.09 percent to 7,659.44 and the Dow down 0.34 percent to 52,002.41. Asian markets were mixed on Monday, with Japan's Nikkei 225 closing 1.02 percent lower at 64,660.20 while South Korea's Kospi recovered from a drop of about 3 percent at the open to close up 0.60 percent at 5,702.30.
Chip stocks and AI financing
The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 3.83 percent on Friday, its eighth straight daily loss, and the main US chip-stock fund lost about 10 percent on the week, its worst week since April 2025. CoreWeave, the debt-funded AI cloud company at the centre of the worries, fell 11.9 percent to 61.39 dollars, and Anthropic is reported to be planning to file publicly for its stock market listing around 26 October.
US rates and the Fed
The 10-year Treasury yield ended the week at about 5.1 percent, and futures on Friday put only about a 15 percent chance on a Federal Reserve rate rise at the meeting that ends on Wednesday 28 October, down from roughly one in three on Thursday. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said he was not signalling a decision, and Fed officials are now in their pre-meeting blackout period.
Energy
December Brent settled on Friday at 109.86 dollars a barrel, up 3.94 dollars from Thursday's 105.92 and its highest settle since 19 May, while November West Texas Intermediate rose 3.44 dollars to 101.41 dollars, up about 10 percent on the week. November WTI expires on Tuesday 20 October, so December WTI, which settled at 99.02 dollars on Friday, becomes the US front month from Wednesday 21 October.
Iran
Britain's maritime monitoring agency says at least 10 vessels were attacked in or near the Strait of Hormuz in the week to Sunday, the most since the war began in February, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two supertankers hit mines on Saturday. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran's seven-day plan to reopen the strait is still on the table if the US lifts its port blockade and oil sanctions, while US Central Command says 97 percent of ships cross without being hit.
Saudi Arabia and Egypt
Egypt's parliament met in closed session on Sunday on President Sisi's request to authorise defensive military support for Saudi Arabia against the Houthis, but postponed the vote until Tuesday 20 October. Saudi-led coalition forces kept intercepting Houthi drones over Riyadh and the Eastern Province during the weekend, and Gulf states condemned the attacks.
Ukraine
Ukraine hit two oil facilities in St Petersburg overnight into Sunday, including the city's main oil terminal, as Russia said it shot down more than 1,000 Ukrainian drones, while Russian strikes on Ukraine's power grid continued. The row between President Zelensky and Trump escalated over the weekend, with Zelensky accusing Trump of pushing Ukraine to capitulate and Trump saying he does not want to drive Russia away from the negotiating table.
European Union
EU leaders ended their Brussels summit on Friday by adopting conclusions on Ukraine, Moldova, the Middle East, defence and migration, after failing on Thursday to agree the 2028 to 2034 budget. The budget talks now pass to the European Council President ahead of a special summit on Thursday 26 November.
China
China's economy grew 4.5 percent in the third quarter from a year earlier, faster than the 4.3 percent of the second quarter and slightly above forecasts of about 4.4 percent, which puts this year's 4.5 to 5 percent target within reach. Stronger growth makes extra stimulus less likely, even though the authorities stepped in last week to prop up a falling stock market.
Indonesia
Indonesia's main stock index fell about another 5 percent on Monday morning to a six-year low, after Friday's 11.57 percent crash on MSCI's proposal to move the country out of its emerging-markets indexes. The financial regulator met market players on Sunday and promised to speed up reforms, but has announced no support measures.
Kenya and Congo (Ebola)
Kenya has confirmed seven cases of the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola linked to the large outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo, including three health workers, although officials say there is no sign of spread in the community. The World Health Organization's latest formal count for Congo is 8,728 cases and 4,205 deaths as of 6 October.
Morocco
King Mohammed VI swore in a new 32-member government on Friday led by Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri, the first woman to head a Moroccan government, after the 23 September parliamentary election. The cabinet includes seven women and a new ministry for artificial intelligence, digital transformation and administrative reform.
Israel and Lebanon
Israeli and Lebanese military delegations meet under US auspices in Tampa, Florida, on Tuesday 20 October, with Lebanon seeking an end to Israeli strikes and Israel demanding that Hezbollah be disarmed. Weekend polls for Israel's election on Tuesday 27 October still show neither Netanyahu's bloc nor the opposition with a clear 61-seat majority.
AI companies
Meta reports third-quarter results on Wednesday 21 October with its call at 22:30 Paris time, and investors will look mainly at whether it raises its AI spending plans after last week's sell-off. Netflix reports on Tuesday 20 October after the US close, and Microsoft and Alphabet follow on Wednesday 28 October.
Nvidia
Nvidia shares fell 1.43 percent to 214.13 dollars on Friday, about 10.5 percent below their 6 October high, in the sector-wide sell-off rather than on company news. Its GTC Berlin conference opens on Tuesday 20 October, with Chief Executive Jensen Huang's keynote on Wednesday 21 October at 11:00 Paris time.
Tesla
Tesla reports third-quarter results on Wednesday 21 October after the US close, with the webcast at 23:30 Paris time, and investors want answers on when Cybercab will be sold, who is liable when a robotaxi crashes and whether spending tops 25 billion dollars. Elon Musk said on Sunday that the Optimus robot and Cybercab will make up the vast majority of Tesla's value, which is his own view.
SpaceX
SpaceX reports third-quarter results on Tuesday 20 October after the US close, with a live audio webcast at 22:30 Paris time, likely giving a first look at quarterly revenue, Starlink subscribers and the price of its Grain spectrum deal. The Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission is targeted for 12:30 Paris time today, after this brief was filed, and the Falcon count for the year stands at 118.
Palantir
Palantir shares fell about 7.2 to 7.4 percent on Friday in the AI and software sell-off, according to secondary reports, although Mizuho and Baird reportedly raised their price targets to 245 and 250 dollars. Its third-quarter results are due on Monday 2 November.
