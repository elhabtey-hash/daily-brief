Cover

Thursday 19 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 18 November morning to Thursday 19 November morning

Asian markets (lead)
Taiwan's main stock index fell 4.4 percent on Thursday, its seventh-largest points drop on record, as doubts about the AI boom and signs of more US rate rises hit chipmakers. South Korea's Kospi still edged up 0.6 percent and Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 0.7 percent.
AI companies
Anthropic, the maker of the Claude AI assistant, priced its share sale at 83 dollars, above its 70 to 78 dollar range, raising about 81.3 billion dollars in the largest stock-market listing on record and valuing it at about 1.5 trillion dollars. Its shares are due to start trading on Nasdaq at 15:30 Paris time today.
Nvidia
Nvidia shares gave back their whole post-results jump on Wednesday and closed down 2.9 percent at 150.96 dollars, even as rivals AMD and Broadcom rose. A new US rule on remote access to advanced chips is due to be published and take effect today.
US markets
Minutes of the Federal Reserve's October meeting showed nearly all officials expect further rate rises, and futures put the odds of a quarter-point rise in December at about 95 percent. The S&P 500 fell for a fifth straight day, the Dow lost 1.1 percent, and the Nasdaq rose 0.4 percent.
Energy
Oil fell on Wednesday after a much larger than expected rise in US crude stocks, with January Brent settling at 91.95 dollars and December West Texas Intermediate at 83.26 dollars, its lowest since late August. Renewed US and Iranian strikes around the Gulf limited the fall.
APEC summit in Shenzhen
Donald Trump met Xi Jinping and, separately, Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, and both sides described the talks as friendly but announced no deals. The first three-way meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders is due later today.
Ukraine and Russia
Ukraine hit the Kerch bridge to Crimea again on Wednesday, and Russia's embassy in The Hague accused the Netherlands of joining the war after a Dutch-based firm said its missiles were used in Tuesday's strike. Ukraine's allies meet in Berlin today with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner.
Europe
The European Commission proposed a new 45 billion euro loan to cover two-thirds of Ukraine's budget gap for 2027, to be repaid only once Russia pays reparations. EU leaders are due to discuss it next week.
Iran and the Gulf
Iran fired drones at a US air base in Kuwait and at targets in Bahrain after a US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island, the first attack on Kuwait in two months. The US military said no American personnel were hurt.
Myanmar
The International Court of Justice in The Hague rules at 10:00 Paris time today on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya Muslim minority. The ruling is binding but the court has no means to enforce it.
India
Cyclone Akvan, a severe cyclonic storm in the Bay of Bengal, is now expected to hit the coast near Gopalpur in Odisha around midday on Friday. Odisha has evacuated about 50,000 people and forecasters have issued a red alert for north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha.
COP31
With one day of scheduled talks left in Antalya, Türkiye, the hosts say 21 issues remain open, including money for poorer countries and the move away from fossil fuels. New draft texts are due today.
AI
Google released Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth new model in seven weeks, which an independent benchmarking firm ranks third behind the best OpenAI and Anthropic models. Google says it matches Anthropic's top model on coding at prices about 25 percent lower.
Tesla
Elon Musk said Tesla and SpaceX would probably be better together but that any merger would need Tesla shareholder approval, and no deal, terms or filing exist. He also spoke of a driverless Model Y for six or seven people.
SpaceX
Cameron County commissioners in Texas voted 3 to 2 not to schedule a hearing on SpaceX's request for a 10-year tax break for a Starbase power plant and data centre. The USSF-317 military launch is set for 05:28 Paris time on Friday.
France
Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu will set out within days the priority topics for a budget compromise with the centre-right and centre-left, his public accounts minister said. The Senate passed the end-of-year spending adjustments for 2026 that the National Assembly had rejected.
Morocco
The UN official winding up the peacekeeping mission in Western Sahara said its withdrawal should be complete by 31 January 2027, as the first troops left Laayoune. Washington repeated its support for Moroccan autonomy as the basis for a settlement.
