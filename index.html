Cover

Tuesday 20 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 19 morning to Tuesday 20 morning

Global markets (lead)
Wall Street steadied on Monday as worries about debt-funded AI spending eased, with the Nasdaq Composite up 1.11 percent to 26,430.89, the S&P 500 up 0.41 percent to 7,690.73 and the Dow down 0.07 percent to 51,966.03. The relief did not reach Seoul on Tuesday, where the Kospi was down about 4 percent in late trade while Japan's Nikkei slipped about 1 percent.
Chip stocks
The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 1.61 percent to 11,316.34 on Monday, its first gain after eight straight losses that cut about 15.7 percent from its value. Meta's results on Wednesday 21 October at 22:30 Paris time are the next test of whether big buyers keep spending on AI chips.
Energy
December Brent settled on Monday at 109.55 dollars a barrel, down 31 cents, and December West Texas Intermediate, the US contract that becomes the front month on Wednesday, fell 48 cents to 98.54 dollars after Trump waived some new sanctions on Russia. The expiring November WTI contract jumped 2.25 dollars to 103.66 dollars ahead of its expiry today.
Iran and Hormuz
Iran's Revolutionary Guards declared the Strait of Hormuz closed and threatened the undersea internet cables that run through it, while US Central Command says commercial ships are still passing. Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir is due in Tehran on Tuesday to try to restart stalled US-Iran talks.
Yemen, Saudi Arabia and Egypt
A Houthi missile strike on a Yemeni government training camp in Shabwa killed at least 53 people on Monday, and the Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi oil sites and airports that have not been independently confirmed. Egypt's parliament again delayed its vote on authorising military support for Saudi Arabia, now until Monday 26 October.
Ukraine
Putin has proposed a meeting with Trump and Xi Jinping at the Asia-Pacific summit in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, and Trump said he was open to it while again attacking Zelensky. Kyiv rejects any talks on its future without it, and Zelensky says US deliveries of Patriot air-defence missiles are being held up.
Israel and Lebanon
Israeli and Lebanese military delegations began US-mediated talks at Central Command in Tampa on Tuesday, expected to run into Wednesday. Lebanon wants Israeli strikes to stop and says it will refuse direct military coordination with Israel, while Israel ties any withdrawal to the disarming of Hezbollah.
Indonesia
Jakarta's main stock index rose about 4 percent in Tuesday's morning session, recovering part of a record two-day fall after MSCI proposed moving Indonesia out of its emerging-markets indexes. The exchange will drop stocks with highly concentrated ownership, about 18 percent of the index's weight, from the index calculation.
France
The National Assembly has postponed Tuesday's formal vote on the tax and revenue part of the 2027 budget because the debate is far from finished. The debate resumes on Tuesday 27 October after the social security budget vote, and the final vote on the whole budget stays set for Tuesday 17 November.
Kenya and Congo (Ebola)
Kenya has confirmed an eighth case of the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola, a hospital attendant exposed to the first imported patient, and all cases so far are linked to that patient. Congo's outbreak has reached eight provinces, with 9,455 confirmed cases and 4,561 deaths reported up to 17 October.
AI companies
Netflix reports third-quarter results on Tuesday at about 22:01 Paris time, and Meta follows on Wednesday 21 October with its call at 22:30 Paris time, where investors will focus on its AI spending plans. Anthropic is reported to be preparing a public listing filing around Monday 26 October, which the company has not confirmed.
Nvidia
Nvidia now says its commitments in Europe total nearly 10 billion dollars, and the German government signed a declaration of intent with it on physical AI at a summit with Chancellor Merz, without giving an amount. Its shares rose 1.38 percent to 217.09 dollars on Monday, and Jensen Huang gives his GTC Berlin keynote on Wednesday 21 October at 11:00 Paris time.
SpaceX
SpaceX reports its third-quarter results after the US close on Tuesday, with a webcast at 22:30 Paris time, and press estimates put revenue at about 12.7 to 13.0 billion dollars. The Bandwagon-5 rideshare launch, scrubbed on Monday, is retargeted for 12:30 Paris time today and had not flown when this brief was filed.
Tesla
Tesla's proxy filing sets its annual meeting for Thursday 3 December with only three items on the ballot and no vote on a SpaceX merger, and shows Elon Musk's 2025 stock awards valued at 158.36 billion dollars. Tesla reports third-quarter results on Wednesday 21 October, with the webcast at 23:30 Paris time.
Palantir
Palantir shares rose 4.36 percent to 194.15 dollars on Monday after it announced a partnership with Knox Systems to speed approval of its software on US government and defence clouds. A Swiss investigation reports that the Swiss military turned Palantir down five times since 2020, which Palantir disputes.
