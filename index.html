Cover

Friday 9 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 8 morning to Friday 9 morning

Iran (lead)
President Donald Trump said on Thursday that the United States will not resume strikes on Iran before the midterm elections on Tuesday 3 November and that talks with Tehran are productive, a day after reports that the White House had asked for strike options. Iran meanwhile appears to be widening its attacks on shipping, with tankers hit off Qatar and near Fujairah outside the Strait of Hormuz.
Energy
Brent crude for December settled on Thursday at 104.28 dollars a barrel, up 4.1 percent, and West Texas Intermediate for November at 91.49 dollars, as Hurricane Isaias shut in about 63 percent of US Gulf of Mexico oil output. Isaias had 105 mile-an-hour winds at 08:00 Paris and is forecast to make landfall on the northern Gulf Coast tonight or early Saturday, and December Brent eased to about 103 dollars in Asia after Trump's Iran remarks.
Ukraine
A Russian glide bomb hit two packed city buses in Kramatorsk on Thursday morning, killing 33 people according to regional authorities, the day after a missile killed 22 in an apartment block in Pryluky. A strike on an energy site also forced emergency power cuts in Kyiv, and the Kremlin said it sees no clear path to peace.
Nobel Prize
The 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature went to the Canadian poet and classicist Anne Carson, 76, for work that the Swedish Academy says creates new forms for contemporary literature in playful dialogue with the classical tradition. The Peace Prize is announced in Oslo at 11:00 Paris today, after this filing, and Economics on Monday.
AI companies
OpenAI told investors its annualised revenue was about 50 billion dollars at the end of September, well below the 68 billion widely reported last month, and AI stocks fell sharply. Nvidia lost about 3 percent, Oracle nearly 6 percent and CoreWeave nearly 8 percent, pulling the Nasdaq down 1.25 percent.
Brazil
The first Datafolha poll of the runoff puts Senator Flávio Bolsonaro on 49 percent and President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on 45 percent, or 52 to 48 percent of valid votes, with a two-point margin of error. The runoff is on Sunday 25 October.
Ethiopia
A conflict monitor says Ethiopian drones struck Eritrean troops near Adigrat in Tigray as they pulled back, after advancing dozens of kilometres into Ethiopia on Wednesday. Ethiopia's foreign minister demanded the immediate withdrawal of Eritrean forces, bringing the two neighbours closer to open war.
SpaceX
SpaceX agreed to buy Grain Management's nationwide 800 megahertz spectrum so that Starlink Mobile can become a US mobile carrier, pending approval by the Federal Communications Commission, which on Tuesday authorised part of a 15,000-satellite direct-to-phone constellation. Shares of AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile fell 7 to 8 percent after hours on the news.
Tesla
The Texas motor vehicle regulator's list of Tesla driverless vehicles jumped by 150 Cybercabs to 319, for 739 vehicles in all, which is the biggest increase since launch, although these are registrations rather than cars giving rides. The federal road-safety regulator has reportedly given Tesla until Friday 30 October to explain how the pedal-less Cybercab meets federal safety standards.
