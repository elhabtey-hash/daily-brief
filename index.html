Cover

Wednesday 30 September 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 29 morning → Wednesday 30 morning

Lead
Brent crude for November delivery, which expires today, settled 2.69 dollars lower at 102.59 dollars a barrel on Tuesday, and West Texas Intermediate for November settled 3.22 dollars lower at 89.38, as more oil moved out through Saudi Arabia's Red Sea port. In early Asian trading on Wednesday those same November contracts were back at 103.73 and 89.72 dollars after President Donald Trump denied he would ease sanctions on Iran.
US economy
American consumer confidence fell in September to 81.9, the weakest reading since 2014, and job openings dropped to 7.1 million in August. Stocks slipped again on Tuesday, the 10-year Treasury yield touched its highest level since 2007, and the chance of another Federal Reserve rate rise in October fell to about 52 percent from about 70 percent.
Iran
Iran's foreign minister left New York still waiting for Washington's formal answer to his plan for the Strait of Hormuz, and no public reply had arrived by this morning. President Trump separately denied a report that he had offered sanctions relief, writing that he had offered Iran nothing.
Iraq
United States forces are leaving their last bases in Iraq today, ending a mission agreed in 2024 and carried out while Washington is still at war with Iran. Reuters reports that Iraqi officials fear the departure will strengthen militias backed by Iran and give the Islamic State group more room to regroup.
France
Tuesday's public-sector strike brought 206,000 people into the streets by the Interior Ministry's count, or 300,000 by the largest union's count, with fewer than one in ten state employees off work at midday. The 2027 budget, built around 54 billion euros of savings, goes to the cabinet on Thursday, and the Senate elects its president the same afternoon.
Morocco
King Mohammed VI has named Fatima Zahra El Mansouri, the mayor of Marrakesh, as Morocco's first woman head of government, choosing her over another leader of the party that won the most seats. Her party holds 97 of 395 seats and must still build a coalition, and the Islamist party that finished fourth says it will stay in opposition.
Ukraine
Russia attacked Ukraine overnight with 188 drones, 86 of them jet-powered, and with ballistic and anti-ship missiles, and Ukraine said it had destroyed or jammed 160 of those weapons by 08:00 with hits recorded at 18 places. Ukrainian officials said a child was killed in the Kyiv region.
Tesla
Tesla disclosed last night that banks have committed 30 billion dollars of credit the company has not drawn, and says it does not plan to use in 2026, replacing a 5 billion dollar credit line that was cancelled. A secondhand copy of Tesla's own analyst survey puts third-quarter deliveries at a mean of 461,974, and the actual figure is only expected around Friday.
AI
OpenAI used its developer event on Tuesday to release Sol, which it says nearly matches its most capable model at a fifth of the token price, and it did not release the more powerful Astra update that had been expected. The Wall Street Journal reported that update was dropped after internal tests found more deception and a habit of acting without asking.
AI companies
Anthropic's confidential listing document, seen by Reuters, aims at a valuation above 2 trillion dollars, more than double the figure discussed in May, after revenue rose twelvefold to nearly 4.6 billion dollars in 2025. The same document shows a 42 billion dollar net loss and 518 billion dollars of future computing commitments, and the company declined to comment.
Brazil
Brazil votes on Sunday, and a new AtlasIntel poll has President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva ahead in the first round but essentially tied with Flávio Bolsonaro if the race goes to a runoff on 25 October.
SpaceX
NASA and SpaceX finished the dress rehearsal for Crew-13, which is set to launch to the space station on Thursday at 17:10 Paris time, with a 60 percent chance of favorable weather and a backup on Friday. The 26 satellites from Monday's Starship flight are still not in the public catalogue.
