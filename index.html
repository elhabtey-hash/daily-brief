Wednesday 7 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Tuesday 6 morning to Wednesday 7 morning

Yemen (lead)
Yemen's government says its Saudi-backed forces have retaken the port of Mocha and control the Bab el-Mandeb Strait at the mouth of the Red Sea, but the Houthis deny losing ground and only the capture of Dhubab has partial independent confirmation. Saudi Arabia confirmed for the first time that Houthi attacks hit its Jazan and Najran airports on Monday evening, wounding three people, while its energy minister said the East-West oil pipeline is again pumping 5.8 million barrels a day.
Nobel Prize
The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics went on Tuesday to Belgian-born Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison, alone, for his decisive role in the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, a cubic kilometre of Antarctic ice that detected the first high-energy neutrinos known to come from far outside our solar system. The Chemistry prize is announced at 11:45 Paris today, after this filing, with Literature on Thursday, Peace on Friday and Economics on Monday 12 October.
Energy
Brent crude for December delivery settled on Tuesday at 100.58 dollars a barrel, up 26 cents, and West Texas Intermediate for November settled at 89.44 dollars, up one cent. Early Wednesday in Asia December Brent rose about 1 percent to near 101.60 dollars as a Gulf of Mexico storm, forecast to become the first Atlantic hurricane of the year, heads for oil platforms and refineries in Louisiana and Mississippi by Friday.
Ukraine
Russia hit Kyiv with waves of missiles and drones overnight into Wednesday, on Vladimir Putin's 74th birthday, killing at least two people in the Darnytskyi district, and strikes also hit Kremenchuk and Zaporizhzhia. Kyiv has accepted American-proposed technical talks with Russia in the United Arab Emirates by the end of October, but Moscow has still not said yes.
Israel
Israel marks three years today since the Hamas-led attack of 7 October 2023, which killed about 1,200 people and took 251 hostages, with memorials at the Nova festival site and across the country. In Gaza more than 74,000 Palestinians have been killed according to local health authorities, and Israeli strikes go on despite the ceasefire.
US markets
The S&P 500 closed above 7,800 for the first time on Tuesday, a record 7,818.93, and the Nasdaq set its second record in two days as the ten-year Treasury yield eased to 5.27 percent from a 24-year high. Minutes of the Federal Reserve's September meeting come out at 20:00 Paris tonight, with the September consumer price report on Wednesday 14 October.
Europe
The European Commission proposed on Tuesday that new members of the European Union serve a 15-year probation in which serious breaches of the rule of law could cost them funding or their votes in the Council. It also set out indicative accession roadmaps for Montenegro, Albania, Moldova and Ukraine.
Tesla
Germany's motor transport authority says Tesla registered 12,552 new cars in September, up 268.7 percent on a year earlier, which puts it back ahead of China's BYD in Germany for the year. Tuesday's European Union vehicle committee discussed Tesla's supervised self-driving software without a vote, and German transport minister Steffen Bilger said he will push for EU-wide approval.
