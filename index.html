Cover

Friday 25 September 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning

Lead
Thursday crude settled sharply higher — Brent November futures at 106.60 dollars a barrel and West Texas Intermediate November at 94.61 — after Saudi forces intercepted Houthi ballistic missiles aimed toward the Yanbu Red Sea export area, even as United States and Iranian negotiators in New York explored an unverified phased path to reopen the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for lifting Washington's economic blockade.
Iran
On the United Nations General Assembly sidelines, sources close to the talks say Washington and Tehran are discussing a staged Hormuz-for-blockade arrangement, while Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi claimed the strait could reopen within seven days if the United States accepts Tehran's conditions. No joint communiqué exists, and senior Iranian commentary still insists the sides remain far apart.
China
After Thursday's White House talks and state dinner, Chinese state media flashed that President Xi Jinping called a new United States–China trade arrangement good news for the global economy, while the concrete bridge already on the record is Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's two-month tariff-truce extension to 10 January 2027. The visit wraps today at the National Archives, with Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang confirmed on the dinner guest list.
Tesla
Tesla completed its invite-only Semi Rollout livestream Thursday evening at Sparks, Nevada, with executives claiming volume-production deliveries to a first set of customers started this week at a factory nameplated near 50,000 trucks a year. List price, cumulative deliveries, line run-rate, and order-book size were not disclosed, so those gaps stay empty rather than invented.
SpaceX
Tomorrow Saturday SpaceX targets Falcon USSF-385 from Vandenberg at about 11:56 Coordinated Universal Time, and Starship Flight 14 remains net Monday 28 September after Thursday's wet dress rehearsal, with the Federal Aviation Administration licence still pending. Falcon year-to-date orbital launches stay at 110 with no overnight flight, and Crew-13 remains firm for Thursday 1 October.
Europe
European Union provisional safeguard measures on grain-oriented electrical steel and related transformer laminations and cores enter force today Friday 25 September, adding tariff-rate quotas and price floors for about 155 days. The step is aimed at heavy import pressure on a strategic grid and transformer input while the full investigation continues.
France
Électricité de France's Flamanville-3 European Pressurised Reactor begins its first full outage of about 350 days tomorrow Saturday 26 September, trimming French nuclear cover into autumn, and partial Senate elections follow on Sunday 27 September. The 2027 budget fight and fuel-support politics remain the domestic overlay into early October.
Ukraine
Russian drones and missiles again hit Kyiv overnight into Friday, damaging residential and commercial buildings and injuring at least one person on preliminary city tallies, a day after a ballistic-missile wave that killed two and wounded eight in the capital. United Nations week continues without a verified energy truce.
