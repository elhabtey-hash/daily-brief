Cover

Tuesday 3 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Monday 2 November morning to Tuesday 3 November morning

US midterms (lead)
Americans vote today for all 435 House seats, 35 Senate seats and 36 governors, and final forecasts make Democrats strong favourites for the House and favourites for the Senate. Polls close from 00:00 to 07:00 Paris time overnight, so no results are in yet and Senate control could take days.
Palantir
Palantir's third-quarter revenue nearly doubled to 2.313 billion dollars and it raised its full-year outlook to about 8.53 billion, well above forecasts. The shares still slipped about 4 percent after hours because new contract signings grew only 6 percent.
Iran and the Gulf
Oil fell more than 7 percent on Monday as Iran and the US signalled openness to a deal through Qatari mediators, though no agreement has been reached. A small US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island overnight, described by US officials as self-defence, shows the conflict is not over.
Energy
January Brent, the headline contract, settled at 104.77 dollars on Monday, down 8.66 dollars, and December WTI, the US front month, settled at 96.46 dollars. It was the biggest one-day fall since July.
Australia
The Reserve Bank of Australia raised its cash rate by a quarter point to 4.85 percent, the highest since 2008, in a split six-to-three vote. It blamed fuel prices from the Middle East war and AI-driven price rises for technology goods.
AI companies
The AI spending worry spread on Monday as the Nasdaq fell 1.07 percent, Oracle dropped 8 percent on a 20 billion dollar stock and bond raise, and Marvell agreed to buy Lumentum in a 115 billion dollar deal. Apple topped analyst estimates but its shares fell about 3 percent after hours on a weaker margin outlook and soft China sales.
Nvidia
Nvidia's own website now lists lower memory and connection speeds for its next Vera Rubin chip than it first announced, though computing power is unchanged. AMD reports tonight, a read on demand for rival AI chips.
Asia markets
Monday's Kospi and Nikkei closes in the last brief are confirmed. Today Seoul's Kospi closed down 3.59 percent on the tech sell-off, and Tokyo was shut for the Culture Day holiday.
Tesla
Tesla opened its robotaxi service without a safety monitor in Fort Worth, its seventh city, and its October registrations rose sharply in several European countries. China's October sales figures are due within days.
SpaceX
Press reports say SpaceX wants to buy the Boost Mobile phone network from EchoStar, which would give its satellite phone service a ground network. The next military launch, USSF-432, slipped to early Saturday Paris time.
France
The National Assembly will not vote on the revenue half of the 2027 budget today as planned, because hundreds of amendments remain. The vote is now expected late this week at the earliest.
Israel
President Herzog begins talks with the parties on Wednesday evening to decide who should try to form a government. No bloc holds the 61 seats needed for a majority.
Ukraine
A Russian strike on an aid centre in Zaporizhzhia killed at least 26 people, after the short Kherson truce ended. The US says it will mediate working-level talks between Ukraine and Russia in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November.
