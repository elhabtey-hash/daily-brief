Cover

Friday 6 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 5 November morning to Friday 6 November morning

Markets (lead)
US stocks plunged on Thursday as investors reassessed the artificial intelligence investment boom, and the selling spread to Japan and South Korea on Friday. The Nasdaq lost 5.52 percent and the semiconductor index fell 12.25 percent.
Japan and South Korea
Tokyo’s Nikkei closed down 5.96 percent at 57,434.77 on Friday, and Seoul’s Kospi fell 6.02 percent to 5,059.69. These are closing figures rather than the larger losses seen during trading.
Energy
January Brent rebounded to a Thursday settlement of 98.29 dollars a barrel as tanker attacks undermined hopes of a quick reopening of Gulf shipping. December West Texas Intermediate settled at 90.52 dollars, also higher.
US economy
The US October jobs report is due at 14:30 CET today, with Reuters’ poll looking for 65,000 new jobs and 4.2 percent unemployment. Thursday’s stock rout pushed back the point at which markets fully price another Federal Reserve rate rise.
United Kingdom
The Bank of England raised its policy rate to 4 percent on Thursday by an eight-to-one vote. It expects inflation to approach 5 percent by mid-2027 and says further decisions depend on energy costs and their effects on wages and prices.
France
France’s Interior Ministry counted about 575,000 protesters on Thursday as living-cost protests added to the government’s budget difficulties. The original 2027 budget goes to the Senate after the Assembly’s 458-to-zero rejection of its revenue section.
Israel
President Isaac Herzog says he will announce his choice to try to form Israel’s next government on Sunday. Gadi Eisenkot received more party recommendations than Benjamin Netanyahu, but a workable coalition is not yet assured.
Paraguay
Paraguayan President Santiago Peña resigned after digital lender ueno’s collapse froze millions of accounts. The crisis has spread from household payments to sovereign bonds and the currency.
AI
OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber for approved security customers on Thursday, reporting stronger vulnerability-finding and scope-control results. Its benchmark and real-world performance figures remain company-reported rather than independently audited.
Capgemini
Capgemini says the European Commission selected it to lead a consortium assessing cyber risks from advanced AI models. Wavestone and France’s national testing laboratory are partners, and no contract value is disclosed.
Nvidia
Nvidia closed Thursday down 9.26 percent at 181.60 dollars as the chip sell-off deepened. CoreWeave’s results on 10 November and Nvidia’s on 17 November will test demand expectations against funding and deployment risks.
Tesla
Tesla’s UK October registrations fell 44.8 percent to 282, while Eku Energy commissioned a 250-megawatt Tesla battery project near Canberra. These are separate demand and energy milestones, not confirmation of any merger or autonomous-service launch.
SpaceX
The Federal Aviation Administration has moved all Starbase environmental scoping meetings online and extended comments to 7 December. SpaceX’s next military Falcon launch is targeted for early Saturday in Paris, not already flown.
