Cover

Friday 27 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 26 November morning to Friday 27 November morning

Palantir (lead)
Poland's defence ministry is due to sign a contract with Palantir at midday today in Warsaw for artificial-intelligence software to support military decisions. The signing is scheduled, not yet confirmed as completed, and the ministry has not given a value or a term.
Markets
US markets reopen this morning for a short session after being closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving, and close early at 19:00 CET. In Asia, South Korea jumped 4.3 percent, Tokyo 2.1 percent and Taiwan 0.4 percent, recovering more of Wednesday's sharp falls.
Energy
January Brent settled at 96.57 dollars a barrel in London on Thursday, up 2.1 percent, as traders kept reacting to a Houthi claim of an attack on Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, which Riyadh has not confirmed, and to reports of further incidents in the Strait of Hormuz. US oil trading was shut on Thursday, so the last official January West Texas Intermediate settle remains Wednesday's 83.68 dollars.
Nvidia
President Trump told the New York Post that a pardon for Nvidia chief Jensen Huang was always an option but probably not necessary, and that he was open to the United States taking an equity stake in the company. Those remarks, from a Tuesday evening interview, were not in the previous three Daily Briefs, and no pardon request or stake talks have been reported.
SpaceX
Elon Musk said every child in America would receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company next month and one from him when his lock-up ends in June, going beyond the company's own statement, which is limited to currently active Trump Accounts and gives no date or share count. SpaceX still has no confirming filing, and two Falcon missions remain listed for Sunday.
Tesla
Texas added 285 Cybercab vehicles to its robotaxi registry, a record one-day increase, taking Tesla's authorised total in the state to 1,841, of which 1,107 are Cybercabs. Being authorised is not the same as being in service, and a European committee meeting on Tesla's assisted-driving software is now formally listed for 15 December, with a possible vote that has still not been confirmed.
Europe
After Thursday's session of a special European Council, President Antonio Costa said 24 of the 27 member states supported a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine in 2027, while Hungary, Slovakia and the Czech Republic still opposed it. Leaders ruled out a deal this week on the bloc's next long-term budget and pointed to December, with the summit's second day running today.
France
Deputies and senators failed on Thursday to agree a common social-security budget for 2027, so the bill returns to the National Assembly for a new reading. The government can still use article 49.3, which lets it adopt a bill without a full vote.
Russia-Ukraine war
President Zelenskyy said on Thursday that Russia is preparing another major attack on Ukraine's energy system, logistics and critical services. The warning comes as European leaders in Brussels discuss a 45 billion euro loan for 2027 and as earlier strikes this week already cut power in several regions.
Iran and the Gulf
Yemen's Houthi movement's claim that it attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, the kingdom's largest, remains unconfirmed by the Saudi government and by the state oil company. Iran has again said mines struck ships in the Strait of Hormuz, and Qatar continues to press for freedom of navigation and a return to talks.
Spain
Today is the last day of campaigning before Spain's general election on Sunday, with a legal day of reflection on Saturday. A pre-election blackout on new voting-intention polls remains in force, and this brief reports no results in advance.
Fed
Futures traders on Thursday evening put the chance of a quarter-point US rate rise on Wed 9 Dec at 99.4 percent, still almost certain after Wednesday's inflation and growth figures. A rise then would be the second rise since September.
