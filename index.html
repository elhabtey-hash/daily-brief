Cover

Friday 13 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Thursday 12 November morning to Friday 13 November morning

Nvidia (lead)
Nvidia said on Thursday that Jensen Huang, its co-founder and only chief executive in 33 years, has resigned, with finance chief Colette Kress as interim chief executive and longtime director Harvey Jones as the first board chair. The company gave no reason, and the shares closed down 4.34 percent at 166.19 dollars after falling as much as 14 percent.
US markets
Wall Street absorbed the Nvidia shock with modest losses, as the S&P 500 fell 0.35 percent while rivals AMD and Broadcom gained almost 6 percent. The 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5 percent and futures still point to a second Federal Reserve rate rise in December, with producer prices due at 14:30 Paris time.
Energy
Oil jumped on Thursday after President Trump said the US no longer needs a deal with Iran, with January Brent settling up 3.3 percent at 95.82 dollars. December West Texas Intermediate settled at 88.74 dollars, and the International Energy Agency publishes its monthly oil report at 10:00 today.
Iran and the Gulf
Trump said the US does not need a deal with Iran and told Vice President Vance to focus on domestic issues, which appears to end the talks channel reported this week. Iran’s military said four tankers were hit by explosions in the Strait of Hormuz after entering waters it calls restricted.
Ukraine
After meeting President Putin in Moscow, US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner told President Zelensky in Dublin that Russia will allow Europe to join the next round of peace talks. Zelensky said the prisoner exchange agreed this week will be the largest of the war, but no date for the next round has been set.
Europe
EU leaders meet in Dublin today for their first summit discussion on artificial intelligence, with Mistral’s chief executive and Geoffrey Hinton as guests. The question of who speaks for the EU in the Ukraine talks is likely to dominate, and no formal decisions are expected.
Japan, Korea and Taiwan
Taiwan rose 1.35 percent and the Nikkei 0.53 percent on Friday as investors treated Huang’s exit as removing uncertainty, while the Kospi fell 0.98 percent. Taiwanese chip designers such as MediaTek jumped on the view that they could gain from Nvidia’s troubles.
China
China confirmed that President Xi will host the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, where he is expected to meet President Trump. Beijing says talks are under way about a possible three-way meeting with President Putin.
Mexico
Trump threatened to double tariffs on Mexico immediately unless it stops more than a thousand migrants walking north toward the US border. Mexico had not publicly responded at filing.
Technology
A Microsoft outage has disrupted Outlook, Teams and other Microsoft 365 services worldwide for about 10 hours, and the company says its fixes are not working as expected. Australia’s cyber security minister said Microsoft does not believe it is a cyberattack.
AI companies
Anthropic says it plans to price its share sale on Wednesday 18 November and start trading on Nasdaq the next day, subject to regulatory clearance. AI cloud providers Nebius and CoreWeave fell sharply on Thursday as investors questioned AI spending after Huang’s exit.
AI
Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster and cheaper model for everyday coding and AI agent tasks that it is rolling out to its Gemini app and Search. Google says it matches its larger Argon model on one independent index, a claim not yet widely tested.
SpaceX
Starship Flight 15 is targeted for 23:45 Paris time tonight, but SpaceX still lists it as pending regulatory approval and no Federal Aviation Administration licence had been seen at filing. Bloomberg reports that talks on a Tesla–SpaceX merger are progressing, but nothing has been agreed or filed.
Tesla
Bloomberg reports that a SpaceX–Tesla merger announcement could come as soon as December, which neither company has confirmed, and Tesla shares rose 1.69 percent. Special committees are still evaluating a deal, with no terms or agreed transaction.
Palantir
Palantir executive Shyam Sankar sold about 64 million dollars of shares on 10 and 11 November under a pre-arranged trading plan, according to his own filings. The Guardian reports that NHS England removed a claim that 137 hospital trusts were benefiting from the Palantir-built data platform.
Morocco
Morocco’s lower house debates and votes on the 2027 budget bill today and tomorrow. Storm Charles caused a blackout across much of Casablanca for about four hours on Wednesday evening.
Science and technology
Researchers used anonymised mobile phone data to show how people moving between towns spread the record Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in Congo for weeks before it was detected. The outbreak has now passed 10,000 confirmed infections and 5,198 deaths.
