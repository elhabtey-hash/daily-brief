Cover

Monday 2 November 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Friday 30 October morning to Monday 2 November morning, including the weekend

AI sell-off (lead)
The rout in technology shares deepened on Friday, with the Nasdaq down 2.86 percent and the S&P 500 down 1.82 percent, as investors questioned the cost of the AI build-out. Futures point to a modest rebound this morning, before Apple and Palantir report this evening.
US midterms
Americans vote on Tuesday 3 November for the whole House and a third of the Senate. Forecasters make Democrats strong favourites to win the House and favourites, less firmly, to win the Senate.
Iran and the Gulf
Tankers were hit near the Strait of Hormuz and off a Saudi Gulf port over the weekend, while Iran says Qatari-mediated talks on ending the war are making progress. Oil is falling this morning on that hope, after Friday's jump to six-month highs.
Energy
December Brent expired on Friday at 117.64 dollars, so January Brent, which settled at 113.43 dollars, is now the headline price. December WTI, the US front month, settled at 104.12 dollars.
Israel
President Herzog opens coalition talks with all parties on Wednesday and is pressing for a unity government to avoid another election. The two Arab-led parties will back former army chief Gadi Eisenkot for prime minister.
Western Sahara
The UN mission in Western Sahara ended at midnight on Saturday after Russia vetoed a US text to extend it and a rival Russian text failed. It is a setback for the UN's role and leaves the US-backed Moroccan autonomy plan as the main track.
Asia markets
Friday's official closes in the last brief are confirmed. Today Seoul's Kospi closed up 2.24 percent on chip buying, while Tokyo's Nikkei slipped 0.33 percent as memory maker Kioxia fell about 15 percent.
Apple and Palantir
Apple reports its July to September quarter this evening, with analysts expecting about 113 billion dollars of revenue and close attention on memory-chip costs. Palantir reports after the US close too, the first test of AI software demand since the sell-off.
Nvidia
Weekend reports say Nvidia is discussing a guarantee on the construction finance for an Nscale data-centre campus anchored by Anthropic. That would deepen its role as a financier of the AI build-out, which investors are now punishing.
Tesla
Tesla's sworn answers to US safety regulators on the Cybercab were due on Friday and nothing has been made public. China's car purchase incentives ended on Saturday, and October sales figures from China and Europe arrive this week.
SpaceX
Starlink 15-18 launched on Saturday morning Paris time and its booster landed safely. The next mission, the military USSF-432 flight, slipped a day to early Wednesday Paris time.
AI
OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast model for operating computers priced at half its predecessor. Google showed Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model.
Ukraine
The six-day local truce around Oleshky in the Kherson region ended on Sunday after Russia declined to extend it. Russian strikes cut power to tens of thousands across Ukraine.
France
The National Assembly votes on the revenue half of the 2027 budget on Tuesday 3 November at 15:00. If the debate on amendments overruns, the vote may slip.
