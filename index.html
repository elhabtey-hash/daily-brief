Thursday 29 October 2026 · Europe/Paris, morning · window Wednesday 28 morning to Thursday 29 morning

Big tech earnings (lead)
Microsoft and Alphabet both grew cloud revenue fast, but Microsoft spent less than planned on data centres because outside suppliers delivered capacity late, while Alphabet said its spending will rise sharply in 2027. Microsoft shares rose about 4 percent after hours and Alphabet's fell more than 7 percent.
US Federal Reserve
The Fed held its main rate at 3.75 to 4.00 percent, but two of the twelve voters wanted a rise, and markets now put the chance of a December increase at about 85 percent. The 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5 percent.
Iran and the Gulf
US officials have told Gulf allies to prepare for possible new strikes on Iran after next Tuesday's midterm elections, although President Trump has not decided. Iran says it is still talking to mediators, and Saudi Arabia intercepted another Houthi missile aimed at its oil region.
Energy
Oil rose on the strike reports, with December Brent settling at 115.32 dollars and December WTI back above 100 dollars at 102.78. December Brent expires on Friday, after which the cheaper January contract, at 111.64 dollars, becomes the headline price.
Israel
With about 91 percent of regular votes counted, Netanyahu's bloc has 53 seats, the Eisenkot-led opposition 48 and Arab-led parties a record 19, so no side has the 61 needed. Absentee ballots are being counted today and are not expected to change the picture much.
Asia markets
Wednesday's official closes are confirmed, and today Seoul's Kospi fell 1.24 percent while Tokyo's Nikkei rose 1.10 percent to a record 64,659.47. Both figures are official closes.
Tesla
Tesla's sworn answers to US safety regulators on whether the Cybercab meets federal safety standards are due tomorrow. A Tesla Semi truck also caught fire on a California motorway without a crash, and the cause is under investigation.
SpaceX
The US Federal Communications Commission votes this afternoon on two proposals to open more radio spectrum for satellite-to-phone service, which matters for Starlink's mobile plans. The vote is still pending.
Palantir
Palantir reports third-quarter results on Monday evening, and a UK minister said no decision has been made on its role in a new 1.8 billion pound military targeting programme.
Nvidia
Nvidia fell 1.2 percent to 215.38 dollars and confirmed it will report results on 17 November. Alphabet's news that it now sells its own AI chips to outside customers is a new competitive test for Nvidia.
Brazil
Brazilian shares fell 2.7 percent on Wednesday as investors worried about Lula's spending plans, and he has not yet named a finance minister.
Morocco
The UN Security Council is expected to vote on Friday or Saturday on a US draft that would make Morocco's autonomy plan the only basis for talks on Western Sahara.
