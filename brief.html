SpaceX
Fact

SpaceX turns a large profit as its AI business overtakes Starlink

What happened

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of 14.58 billion dollars, up 161 percent from 5.58 billion a year earlier, with operating income of 2.79 billion dollars, net income of 4.4 billion and adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) of 8.8 billion (SEC 8-K, 20 Oct). Its AI and computing segment, called Super Intelligence, brought in 7.56 billion dollars and is now the biggest by revenue. Starlink consumer subscribers rose to 13.6 million from 12.0 million in June, with average monthly revenue per user of 69 dollars. Capital spending was 19.6 billion dollars in the quarter. Shares rose about 4 percent after hours (Reuters). On the call, the finance chief guided first-quarter 2027 revenue to 33 to 37 billion dollars and fourth-quarter capital spending to 35 to 40 billion, and Musk said Starship Flight 15 will try to catch both the booster and the ship next month.

Context (what it sits on)

Revenue was well above the roughly 12.7 to 13.0 billion dollars expected in press reports. The jump comes mostly from renting computing power, which Musk said customers want several times over. Spending on data centres far exceeds cash generated, which is why SpaceX is reported to be seeking about 40 billion dollars of debt. Musk said he would be surprised if SpaceX added less than 13 gigawatts of computing by end-2027, an expectation rather than formal guidance. The Bandwagon-5 rideshare flew on Tuesday, taking Falcon launches this year to 119.

Forward (months–years)

Lockup expiries are reported to free about 1.30 billion shares on Thursday 22 October and about 329 million on Saturday 24 October (press, no filing). Starlink 15-15 is targeted for 01:02 Paris on Thursday 22 Oct. The quarterly 10-Q filing is due by about Monday 9 November.

Tesla
Mixed

Tesla and SpaceX boards study a possible merger

What happened

On SpaceX's results call Musk said the Tesla and SpaceX boards have each formed special committees of independent directors to discuss a possible combination. SpaceX's statement says the committees have not reached agreement on terms and there is no guarantee they will. Musk said he is conflicted and excluded, but called the idea a good one. Any deal would need approval from shareholders of both companies. Tesla shares rose 1.8 percent after hours. Reuters, citing a source, says talks started in recent weeks and could see SpaceX acquire Tesla.

Context (what it sits on)

Tesla has not yet filed an 8-K on the committee. Its proxy for the Thursday 3 December annual meeting has no merger item. Barclays had suggested a deal could come before that meeting, which is speculation. Musk also said the Tesla-SpaceX chip venture Terafab expects a first test chip around May to June 2027 and volume output in the third quarter of 2028, with large uncertainty.

Forward (months–years)

Tesla's third-quarter results webcast is tonight, Wednesday 21 October, at 23:30 Paris time, and merger questions are expected. Results are not yet out. A Tesla filing could come any day.

Iran and Hormuz
Mixed

Iran says tankers hit mines and holds out its seven-day plan

What happened

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three tankers struck naval mines while using southern routes through the Strait of Hormuz that Iran calls unauthorised, with fires on board (Malay Mail, Middle East Eye). Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran's seven-day proposal remains open: if Washington accepts its conditions, the strait would reopen on day six and talks would continue on day seven (Gulf News). Iran's conditions include ending the US blockade of its ports and easing oil sanctions. Tracking sites show commercial crossings at roughly 1 to 2 percent of normal.

Context (what it sits on)

The US says it controls the waterway and has cleared mines, so the two sides give opposite accounts. Pakistan's army chief is acting as a go-between. Washington wants nuclear concessions, which Iran has not offered.

Forward (months–years)

Signal is any US reply to the plan, or ship counts rising.

Nvidia
Claim

Chinese giants order millions of Nvidia chips, keynote today

What happened

Reuters reports that Alibaba, ByteDance and Tencent ordered more than 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards, worth over 35 billion dollars at list price, and that total Chinese orders exceed 6 million units, but Nvidia has warned it may deliver only 5 to 10 percent. A 70 billion dollar figure circulating online is not in the Reuters report. Nvidia closed at 216.47 dollars, down 0.29 percent, then rose about 2.1 percent after hours to about 221 dollars after SpaceX said demand for computing far exceeds supply. CoreWeave rose about 5 percent and Nebius about 6 percent after hours.

Context (what it sits on)

The RTX PRO 5500 is a chip designed to meet US export rules for China. Supply limits in memory and wafers mean the orders may yield only a few billion dollars of revenue. A Reuters Breakingviews column called Nvidia the central bank of AI for propping up its own customers.

Forward (months–years)

Jensen Huang's GTC Berlin keynote runs 11:00 to 13:00 Paris time today and has not happened yet. DGX Spark 64GB goes on sale on Friday 23 October. Nvidia reports on Tuesday 17 November.

Palantir
Claim

Pressure in Europe from investors and campaigners

What happened

The Financial Times reports that HSBC Asset Management is weighing whether to drop Palantir from its roughly 9 billion dollar Islamic Global Equity Index fund after questions from UK pension schemes Nest and People's Pension. No decision has been made. Separately, the campaign group Foxglove published a report alleging that uptake figures for the NHS Federated Data Platform are unreliable and that Palantir lobbied ahead of a ministerial decision.

Context (what it sits on)

Neither item affects revenue directly, but both add to ethical pressure on Palantir in Europe as the UK decides on the NHS platform by December.

Forward (months–years)

Palantir holds a Sovereign AI event in Seoul on Thursday 22 and Friday 23 October and reports on Monday 2 November, guiding revenue of 2.160 to 2.164 billion dollars against about 2.18 billion expected.
