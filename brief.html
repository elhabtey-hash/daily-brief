Nvidia (lead)
Fact

Third-quarter results beat forecasts and the outlook is raised

What happened

Nvidia, the leading maker of AI chips, reported results for its fiscal third quarter (August to October) at about 22:20 CET on Tuesday (press release and Form 8-K). Revenue was $115.8 billion, up 103 percent on a year earlier, against about $109.1 billion expected by analysts (LSEG). Data-centre revenue was $107.6 billion. Adjusted earnings per share were $2.68 against $2.48 expected. For the current quarter, which has 14 weeks instead of 13, Nvidia guided to $130 billion plus or minus 3 percent (consensus about $125.1 billion), assuming no data-centre computing sales to China. Gross margin is guided to 71 to 72 percent, which interim chief executive and chief financial officer Colette Kress called the low point, with 72 to 73 percent next fiscal year.

Context (what it sits on)

On the call Kress raised the outlook for revenue growth next fiscal year to about 75 percent from 70 percent, and said Rubin, the new chip generation, brought in nearly $20 billion in its first quarter of shipments. She also said Nvidia is no longer shipping licensed Hopper data-centre chips to China; those sales were $2.4 billion in the quarter. On the new US rule that requires export licences for advanced chips sold to five more countries, she said demand still far exceeds supply. Part of the jump in next-quarter guidance comes from the extra week (an analyst estimate of $9 to 10 billion is a claim).

Forward (months–years)

Results confirm that spending on AI data centres is still rising fast, which matters for chip suppliers in Taiwan, Korea and Japan and for the AI companies beat. The 10-Q quarterly filing is not yet out. Watch the first regular US trading session after the results (15:30 CET today) and the staff all-hands meeting at 18:00 CET.

Nvidia (lead)
Fact

Board shuts the door on Huang, and Congress dates are set

What happened

Board chair Harvey Jones said on the call that Jensen Huang voluntarily resigned after the disclosure of personal behaviour inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, and that the board unanimously accepted the resignation and does not intend to reinstate him. Huang resigned per the Form 8-K of 12 November, effective 11 November. The board has hired the search firm Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent chief executive, and created a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson (company release, 17 November).

Context (what it sits on)

This is the first company statement giving a reason for the departure; the 8-K was silent. Huang said earlier on Tuesday he would be honoured to return if asked (a claim, reported by CNBC and NBC). The House Foreign Affairs Committee said Huang and general counsel Tim Teter agreed to closed-door transcribed interviews on Tue 1 Dec and Wed 2 Dec. A Senate Banking Committee appearance by Huang on Thu 3 Dec is still only a request. No subpoena has been issued.

Forward (months–years)

The new committee is aimed at Washington, where export controls and the Huang inquiry overlap. Watch for a permanent chief executive shortlist, any separation-terms filing, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's request for answers from the board within about ten days (around Wed 25 Nov), which is a political demand, not a legal deadline.

US markets
Fact

Fourth day of losses before Nvidia; retail sales steady

What happened

Official Tuesday closes: the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 848.75 points, or 1.64 percent, to 50,759.42; the S&P 500 fell 1.03 percent to 7,340.53, its lowest close since late July; the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.74 percent to 24,328.38, after touching its lowest level since April (CNBC, AP). Prior closes were 51,608.17, 7,416.59 and 24,509.76. Context (what it sits on)

October retail sales rose 0.2 percent, matching forecasts, after 0.8 percent in September (Census Bureau). Separately, the Pan American Health Organization said the US has lost its measles elimination status after 26 years.

Forward (months–years)

Strong spending supports the Fed's case for raising rates. Futures prices on the CME FedWatch tool imply a quarter-point rise in December is close to certain (about 93 to 96 percent in snapshots on 17 November). The Fed raised rates in September, so a rise in December would be a second increase this cycle.

Fact

Oil edges up on Gulf strikes, still down sharply this month

What happened

Forward (months–years)

Ukraine and Russia
Mixed

Kerch bridge road link cut

What happened

Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the Kerch bridge, which links Russia to occupied Crimea, after two strikes in two days (Reuters verified videos; Ukrainian general staff). President Volodymyr Zelensky called it an unprecedented operation. The Kremlin-appointed head of Crimea confirmed road traffic is suspended; the rail line is still standing with a damaged arch, and trains resumed after a pause. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the bridge will be repaired.

Context (what it sits on)

The bridge, opened by Putin in 2018, is Russia's main symbol of its 2014 annexation of Crimea and a supply route. Photos suggest a road arch of about 227 metres fell into the strait and may block the shipping channel to the Sea of Azov (Guardian, a claim). Russian strikes hit two Kyiv bridges on Monday. At a NATO meeting, Pentagon policy chief Elbridge Colby called Ukraine's deep strikes counterproductive; German defence minister Boris Pistorius and UK defence secretary Wes Streeting rejected that.

Forward (months–years)

The strike strengthens Kyiv's hand before Thursday's Trump, Xi and Putin meeting in Shenzhen and widens a US-Europe rift over how Ukraine fights. Watch for Russian retaliation on Ukrainian energy and bridges and whether repair work is targeted again. US envoys say talks on a deal are progressing but problems remain (claim).

Mixed

What happened

Europe
Fact

Commission autumn forecast and budget warning to France and Belgium

What happened

The European Commission's autumn forecast sees EU growth of 1.4 percent in 2026 and 2027 and 1.5 percent in 2028; euro area growth of 1.2, 1.2 and 1.3 percent. EU inflation is forecast at 3.3 percent this year and 3.2 percent next, easing to 2.3 percent in 2028. Euro area inflation reached 4 percent in October and is expected to peak above 4 percent around the turn of the year. Economy commissioner Valdis Dombrovskis said France and Belgium must cut deficits and debt; France's deficit is forecast at 5.5 percent of GDP this year and its debt at 119.2 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

EU rules aim for deficits of no more than 3 percent of output. Dombrovskis said he sees no imminent financial stability problem. Separately, the Commission preliminarily found the Chinese marketplace Temu in breach of the Digital Services Act (DSA) over illegal and unsafe products; a final decision could bring a fine of up to 6 percent of global turnover. It also said it has approved more than 20 state aid decisions for new chip plants, with over €33 billion of investment.

Forward (months–years)

Higher inflation makes further European Central Bank rate rises more likely and squeezes budgets. Watch French budget votes (France beat) and Temu's response.

France
Fact

Senate restores 2023 pension reform; Le Pen sets out her plan

What happened

Just after midnight on 17 November the Senate, where the right and centre hold a majority, voted 200 to 129 to reinstate the 2023 pension reform, which raises the legal retirement age to 64, against the government's position (Public Sénat). The reform was suspended under last year's social security budget deal with the Socialists. On Tuesday Marine Le Pen of the far-right National Rally said she would abolish the 2023 reform from 1 January 2028, returning the legal age to 62 or even 60, and did not rule out a referendum (AFP).

Context (what it sits on)

The Senate vote is a first reading and is unlikely to survive in the National Assembly. Labour minister Jean-Pierre Farandou said the government would not change its position. A public sector strike on Tuesday drew 146,000 marchers according to the Interior Ministry and more than 500,000 according to the CGT union. Foreign minister Jean-Noël Barrot said France is not a co-belligerent in Ukraine.

Forward (months–years)

Pensions are shaping up as a central issue of the 2027 presidential race. Watch the social security budget's return to the Assembly and the Commission's warning on France's deficit (Europe beat).

Morocco
Mixed

What happened

US ambassador to the UN Mike Waltz met foreign minister Nasser Bourita in Rabat on 17 November. Waltz said the status quo in Western Sahara is unsustainable, restated US recognition of Moroccan sovereignty, and urged the Polisario Front, the independence movement backed by Algeria, to return to the ceasefire (Moroccan and Spanish press). Bourita said the two countries had worked on joint proposals and that there is no solution outside Moroccan sovereignty and its autonomy plan.

Context (what it sits on)

The UN mission, MINURSO, is being wound up after Russia vetoed its renewal at the end of October; Waltz said the US draft had 11 votes in the Security Council. Waltz also met the UN official overseeing the withdrawal.

Forward (months–years)

Watch for any reported fighting along the berm, Algeria's response and whether the joint proposals are published.

Science and technology
Fact

Voyager 1 reaches one light day; Alzheimer device fails

What happened

NASA's Voyager 1, launched in 1977, reaches one light day from Earth, about 25.9 billion kilometres, at 5:16 a.m. US Eastern time, or 11:16 CET today, the farthest any human-made object has travelled (Nature). In medicine, a 673-person trial of Spectris AD, a headset using flickering light and sound to stimulate the brain, found no cognitive benefit in Alzheimer's disease after a year compared with a sham device (Cognito Therapeutics, reported by Nature).

Context (what it sits on)

A radio signal now takes a full day to reach Voyager. The Alzheimer company still plans to seek US approval next year based on a subgroup with mild impairment, which independent experts call exploratory.

Forward (months–years)

The failed trial is a setback for the idea of treating Alzheimer's by boosting gamma brain waves.
