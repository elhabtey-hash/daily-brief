Nvidia (lead)
Mixed

Board investigation and chief executive apology

What happened

Nvidia’s board said on Sunday 8 November that a committee of independent directors, with outside counsel, will investigate recent reporting that Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. It says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, that Huang supports the inquiry, and that it has confidence in him. In a message to employees, Huang says he was unfaithful to his wife, apologises to family, colleagues, the board, partners and shareholders, and asks for his family’s privacy. Reuters reports shares about 3.4 percent lower at roughly $180 in out-of-hours trading versus Friday’s $186.64 close.

Context (what it sits on)

The intelligence-targeting allegations come from press reports (New York Times, Wall Street Journal, Financial Times) and remain claims. China denies them. The House China select committee, Foreign Affairs and Intelligence committee chairs had already asked, in a letter dated 4 November, for a closed-door briefing on 18 November about Huang’s contacts with an executive of the company Megaspeed and Nvidia’s security protocols. Huang remains chief executive. Separately, Nvidia, Korean chip designer Rebellions and SK Telecom announced a Korean sovereign-AI collaboration using Nvidia’s NVLink Fusion connection technology.

Forward (months–years)

Wed 11 Nov: deadline to accept or propose another date for the House briefing. Tue 17 Nov around 22:20 CET: third-quarter results, call 23:00 CET. Wed 18 Nov: requested briefing. Nvidia AI Day Seoul runs through Tue 10 Nov. Watch for board updates, not speculation beyond the company statements.

US economy
Fact

Strong jobs report lifts December hike odds

What happened

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on Friday 6 November that payrolls rose 165,000 in October against a Reuters poll of 65,000. Unemployment fell to 4.1 percent from 4.2 percent. September was revised up to 60,000 from 29,000 and August to 186,000, a combined 84,000 extra jobs. Average hourly earnings rose 0.4 percent on the month and 3.1 percent on the year. US stocks closed Friday mixed: S&P 500 7,376.86, up 0.31 percent, Nasdaq Composite 24,579.42, up 1.09 percent, Dow 51,285.73, down 0.46 percent. For the week the S&P 500 lost 2.7 percent and the Nasdaq 3.4 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

Reuters reported fed funds futures pricing about an 80 percent chance of a quarter-point rise at the 8–9 December meeting after the report, up from about 75 percent before it. That would be a second rise after September’s. Pay growth of 3.1 percent trails September consumer inflation of 3.7 percent, so households still feel squeezed. Long-term unemployment rose to its highest since 2021, a weak spot in a strong headline.

Forward (months–years)

Tue 10 Nov 14:30 CET: October consumer prices. Fri 13 Nov 14:30 CET: producer prices. Wed 11 Nov is the Veterans Day holiday. Market odds are a snapshot, not a Fed decision.

Energy
Fact

Oil steadies after a historic weekly drop

What happened

January Brent settled Friday at $98.32, up 3 cents from Thursday’s $98.29. December WTI settled at $90.83, up 31 cents from $90.52 (Reuters, Xinhua). January Brent is the headline contract and December WTI is the front month until it expires around 20 November. EnergyNow, cited by Catapult Commodities, puts the January Brent contract at $113.43 a week earlier, a fall of about 13 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

Prices fell on expectations that the midterm result makes a US-Iran settlement more likely, and Reuters says spot premiums for Middle East crude are back to pre-conflict levels. Mines, ship strikes and the US blockade of Iranian ports still restrict Gulf flows. No Monday intraday quote is presented as a settlement.

Forward (months–years)

Watch Iran talks through Qatar, the US inflation data on Tuesday and any confirmed reopening of Hormuz shipping.

Iran and the Gulf
Mixed

Trump plays down new strikes as ships are hit

What happened

Trump told CNN on Sunday that new attacks were highly unlikely if Iran signs “a very strong agreement,” while warning that the US has identified infrastructure targets. He later said a new campaign, Task Force Iron Gate, against Iranian weapons movements over land borders and the Caspian Sea is in effect. Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said two tankers hit mines in Hormuz, and Fars said a third caught fire. The US said it disabled a vessel trying to break its blockade in the Gulf of Oman. Saudi Arabia said falling debris from an attempted attack damaged a Saudi Aramco building in Riyadh, with no injuries.

Context (what it sits on)

Reuters could not confirm the reported strikes. Qatar denies reports that talks failed. Iran has sent a nuclear proposal through Qatar but conditions talks on seven demands, including sanctions relief, frozen assets and reparations, which Rubio called unrealistic. Vice President JD Vance said the war cost Republicans the election.

Forward (months–years)

No deal, ceasefire or reopening of the strait is written as achieved. Watch whether Washington answers Iran’s proposal and whether shipping incidents decline.

Israel
Fact

Eisenkot gets the first chance to form a government

What happened

Herzog formally tasked Yashar party leader Gadi Eisenkot at a ceremony on Sunday 8 November, after factions representing 67 lawmakers recommended him and 46 recommended Benjamin Netanyahu. Eisenkot has 28 days, extendable by 14. He called for a national emergency government and said Likud and the Religious Zionist Party were welcome to join.

Context (what it sits on)

His four-party alliance won 48 seats and has ruled out governing with Arab-led parties. Adding the two ultra-Orthodox parties would give exactly 61 of 120 seats, according to Al Jazeera’s count. Netanyahu stays caretaker prime minister and would likely be next in line if Eisenkot fails.

Forward (months–years)

Coalition talks start now. A mandate is not a government, and no coalition is presumed.

Poland
Fact

Kraków market square attack treated as terrorism

What happened

A 24-year-old man drove into Kraków’s main square on Saturday afternoon and opened fire, killing six people, including a British man and an Austrian woman, and injuring 17, the Guardian reports citing police and officials. Police found his parents and sister dead at his home. The national prosecutor opened a terrorism investigation. AP and the BBC report the suspect later died in hospital.

Context (what it sits on)

The interior minister says the suspect held ultra-nationalist, far-right views. Mass shootings are rare in Poland, and the attack follows school stabbings in October that officials linked to extremist online content. Two days of national mourning began on Sunday.

Forward (months–years)

Watch the investigation’s findings on motive and how the weapons were obtained.

Paraguay
Fact

President removed, successor sworn in

What happened

Correction to Friday’s brief, which said Peña resigned: Congress rejected his offered resignation. The Chamber of Deputies voted 78–0 to impeach him on Thursday and the Senate removed him on Friday 6 November with 44 votes and one abstention, according to Al Jazeera and AP. Vice President Pedro Alliana was sworn in and serves until August 2028. Prosecutors ordered Peña held under house arrest on suspicion of breach of trust and other offences.

Context (what it sits on)

Ueno had about three million customers. About $355 million of state pension money was invested in it. At least 27 people face charges and an arrest warrant was issued for the former central bank head. Removal rather than resignation denies Peña the life Senate seat and legal protection a former president would receive.

Forward (months–years)

Watch depositor and pension access and the new cabinet. Suspicions against Peña are not court findings.

China
Fact

Factory prices surge, consumer prices soften

What happened

The National Bureau of Statistics said on Monday that consumer prices rose 0.7 percent from a year earlier in October, down from 0.9 percent and below the 0.8 percent expected. Producer prices rose 5.7 percent, up from 5.0 percent and above the 5.3 percent Reuters poll, the fastest in about four and a half years.

Context (what it sits on)

Higher energy costs from the Gulf conflict are lifting industrial prices while household demand stays weak. That squeezes manufacturers’ margins and complicates any decision on more stimulus.

Forward (months–years)

Tue 10 Nov: October trade data. Mid-November: activity data. Watch whether export strength holds.

SpaceX
Mixed

NASA lunar awards and a busy launch week

What happened

NASA said on Saturday 7 November it selected SpaceX for a Cislunar Communications Deployment Demonstration: a Starship will deliver communications satellites to the region between Earth and the Moon in 2028, value undisclosed. SpaceX is also one of eight companies eligible for NASA’s second Commercial Lunar Payload Services contract, which has a shared $10 billion ceiling, not a $10 billion award. USSF-432 launched on 6 November Pacific time, so Falcon launches this year now stand at 122. A filing shows director Donald Harrison resigned on 4 November, not because of any disagreement.

Context (what it sits on)

Cochise County, Arizona, will hold a planning hearing on 9 December on a roughly 747-acre AI data-centre campus filed by Tombstone Resources. The county notice does not name SpaceX, and the link is secondary reporting. A special committee is evaluating a potential Tesla–SpaceX combination, with no agreed transaction.

Forward (months–years)

Tue 10 Nov 04:03–08:03 CET: USSF-627 Falcon 9. Fri 13 Nov 05:23 CET: USSF-317. Fri 13 Nov 23:45 CET: Starship Flight 15 target, pending regulatory approval. Today: Federal Communications Commission (FCC) direct-to-device comments due.
