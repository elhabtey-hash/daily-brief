AI sell-off becomes a global shock

What happened

Thursday 5 November: the S&P 500 closed at 7,353.73, down 4.36 percent, the Nasdaq Composite at 24,315.00, down 5.52 percent, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average at 51,523.06, down about 3.44 percent (AP closing table via search). Jobs pending as Fed pricing shifts

Reuters’ currency report quoted CME FedWatch at 66.1 percent odds of a December rise, while its later closing commentary said another rise was not fully priced until March. United Kingdom
Fact

Bank of England raises Bank Rate to 4 percent

What happened

The Monetary Policy Committee raised Bank Rate from 3.75 to 4 percent. Eight members supported that step and Catherine Mann wanted 4.25 percent. The Bank’s official minutes say September consumer-price inflation was 3.5 percent and project about 5 percent by mid-2027.

Context (what it sits on)

This resolves the event that was pending in yesterday’s brief. The Bank is responding to the duration of the energy shock even though wider wage-price spillovers remain limited. Governor Andrew Bailey sees a case for another rise without evidence of softer inflation pressure, but policy is not pre-set.

Forward (months–years)

Next decision 17 December, outside the main 30-day horizon. Lower oil would need to persist to change the inflation outlook.

What happened

Reuters reports Peña resigned Thursday before impeachment proceedings advanced, after losing support within his Colorado Party. Ueno served around three million customers and administered about $850 million in public funds. The guarani closed down nearly five percent at 6,490 per dollar, and sovereign bonds fell. Brazil
Mixed

Rate-cut cycle stops after Lula’s reelection

What happened

Reuters’ Thursday analysis follows Wednesday’s decision to hold the Selic rate at 13.75 percent. The central bank raised its relevant-horizon inflation forecast to 3.5 percent from 3.1 percent. Some economists now expect a hold through 2027 or longer, while the yield curve implies renewed increases early next year.

Context (what it sits on)

Forward (months–years)

Watch ministerial appointments and concrete fiscal measures rather than treating market-implied hikes as announced decisions.

Europe
Claim

EU budget levy compromise floated

What happened

POLITICO cites two diplomats on a proposal for a 0.05 percent levy on firms with turnover above €750 million, replacing a lump-sum scheme starting at €100 million. Other ideas include more waste-levy revenue and a larger EU share of carbon-border tax receipts. A negotiating document is reported for 21 November.

Context (what it sits on)

The dispute concerns EU own resources, meaning revenue paid directly into the EU budget, and how to balance national contributions with common spending. The discussions are bargaining positions, not adopted taxes.

Forward (months–years)

Bilateral talks continue into Monday. Watch the reported 21 November document and the following week’s special summit.

AI
Mixed

Cybersecurity model raises capability and safety stakes

What happened

OpenAI says GPT-6 Cyber scores 48.5 percent on its ExploitGym evaluation versus 32.1 percent for its predecessor. It reports finding 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and developing exploits for 948 Linux kernel vulnerabilities. Initial availability is limited to selected approved Daybreak Red customers through its interface and programming access.

Context (what it sits on)

This is a model-capability and safety story, separate from the AI infrastructure financing beat. Better vulnerability discovery can help patch software but also increases misuse risk. The supplier’s evaluation of staying within authorised scope does not prove safety in every deployment.

Forward (months–years)

Watch independent testing, vendor patch confirmations and the conditions for expanded access. Do not confuse a restricted rollout with general availability.
