Saudi Arabia
Fact

Houthi missile hits Aramco building in Dhahran after coalition strikes Sanaa fuel depots. Four hurt in Riyadh

What happened

On Tuesday 13 October the Saudi-led coalition struck the Yemen Petroleum Company's al-Sabaha fuel depot near Sanaa and other Houthi-held oil sites. The Houthis replied with missiles and drones aimed at Riyadh, Dammam and other cities. Saudi Arabia's energy ministry said a ballistic missile hit an Aramco administrative building in Dhahran, causing a limited fire and damage to upper floors with no casualties reported, and debris from intercepted missiles wounded four people in Riyadh and damaged 15 homes (AP, Al Jazeera, CNA, BBC).

Context (what it sits on)

Saturday's strike on Riyadh airport killed 12. Washington provides intelligence and refuelling but has not struck Yemen. Hitting Aramco property moves the war from airports towards oil infrastructure, the thing oil markets fear most.

Forward (months–years)

Watch whether the Houthis hit production or export sites such as Abqaiq or Ras Tanura, a US decision on joining strikes, and Red Sea shipping.

Iran
Mixed

Two more ships hit near Hormuz. Iranian officials say tactics changed to raise hit rate before US midterms

What happened

The UK Maritime Trade Operations centre (UKMTO) reported a tanker and a container ship struck by 'unknown projectiles' north of Oman's Musandam peninsula on Tuesday 13 October, with no names, times or damage given (UKMTO via Maritime Executive, Iran International). Two Iranian officials told Reuters the method and pace of attacks had changed to raise the hit rate, with the aim of keeping oil prices high and pressuring Washington before the 3 November midterms. A tally of UKMTO warnings shows at least 12 tankers attacked in and around the strait in a week, including six ships on Sunday, the most in one day since the war began in February (The National, Al-Monitor). India says two Indian crew are missing.

Context (what it sits on)

Iran has restricted Hormuz since the US-Israeli strikes began, and the US blockades Iranian ports. Trump has pledged no US strikes on Iran before the midterms, which Tehran appears to be exploiting.

Forward (months–years)

Watch the daily UKMTO count, war-risk insurance rates and any US naval escort change.

Energy
Fact

Tue settle: Brent Dec $105.69 (-0.96), WTI Nov $94.14 (+0.40). Gulf shut-in down to 16.13%

What happened

Tuesday 13 October settlements. Brent for December delivery, the front month, settled at 105.69 dollars a barrel, down 96 cents or 0.9 percent from Monday's December settle of 106.65. West Texas Intermediate (WTI) for November delivery, the front month, settled at 94.14 dollars, up 40 cents or 0.4 percent from Monday's November 93.74, its highest close since 24 September (Reuters via Global Banking & Finance, Newsquawk, PFL Petroleum). Both changes tie exactly. Hopes the US-Russia diesel deal frees up Russian supply weighed on Brent, while Gulf attacks kept a floor under prices. US diesel futures rose about 4.5 percent (stockminded, Claim). Wednesday Asia quotes are intraday and not used. Hurricane Isaias: the US offshore regulator reported on 13 October that 329,059 barrels a day of Gulf oil, 16.13 percent of output, and 355 million cubic feet a day of gas, 16.59 percent, remained shut in, with five platforms and one rig evacuated, down from 29.63 percent of oil on Monday (bsee.gov).

Context (what it sits on)

The gap between Brent December and WTI November narrowed from about 12.91 to 11.55 dollars as Gulf-exposed grades gave back some risk premium. The Gulf of Mexico restart is well advanced.

Forward (months–years)

WTI November expires Tuesday 20 October, Brent December Friday 30 October. OPEC Plus meets Sunday 1 November. Watch Aramco security and Hormuz.

Ukraine
Mixed

Russia spares energy sites for first time since Trump's truce call, but hits cities. Kremlin still noncommittal

What happened

Russia's defence ministry summary of overnight strikes into Tuesday 13 October made no mention of Ukrainian energy targets, the first such night since Trump announced an energy ceasefire on Sunday, while ballistic missiles and drones hit transport and logistics and killed at least four people in Kharkiv. A missile fell about a kilometre from a heating plant in Lviv (Reuters via France 24, Straits Times). Ukraine says Russia hit six energy sites on Monday. Zelensky said Ukraine had made no strikes on Russian refineries for two days. The Kremlin said contacts are ongoing with 'no change to the situation yet' (TASS), and Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov said Zelensky 'does not deserve a respite'. Trump insists both sides agreed.

Context (what it sits on)

It is unclear whether Russia deliberately held off. The truce is tied to a US sanctions waiver on Russian diesel, which Europe opposes.

Forward (months–years)

Watch whether a second night passes without energy strikes, and the European Council on Thursday 15 and Friday 16 October.

China
Fact

September exports +36.3%, imports +31%, surplus $133.5bn. Nine-month surplus $938bn

What happened

China's customs data on Wednesday 14 October showed September exports of 447.2 billion dollars, up 36.3 percent from a year earlier, and imports of 313.7 billion, up 31 percent, both far above forecasts, for a monthly surplus of 133.5 billion dollars against 119.1 billion in August (AP, CNA, FXStreet). January to September exports rose 21.3 percent and the surplus reached 938.1 billion dollars. In yuan terms exports rose 29.6 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

Part of the jump reflects a weak base and higher prices for traded goods. A record surplus feeds complaints from the EU and others about Chinese overcapacity.

Forward (months–years)

September consumer and producer prices and third-quarter growth follow in the coming days. Watch EU trade-defence talk at the summit.

US markets
Fact

Banks mixed: JPMorgan misses, Goldman and Citi beat. S&P 500 flat at 7,805.45. CPI today 14:30 Paris

What happened

JPMorgan reported third-quarter net income of 15.3 billion dollars, 5.57 dollars a share, up 6 percent but below the 5.93 forecast cited by AP, including 658 million dollars of securities losses (JPMorgan release on sec.gov). Goldman Sachs earned 17.01 dollars a share on revenue of 18.68 billion, up 23 percent. Citigroup beat forecasts with revenue of about 23.8 billion. Wells Fargo beat on profit but missed on revenue and net interest income. On Tuesday the S&P 500 slipped 0.02 percent to 7,805.45, the Dow fell 0.35 percent to 51,670.10 and the Nasdaq 0.15 percent to 27,204.38 as chip stocks fell a fifth day (AP, ts2.tech). The ten-year Treasury yield was near 5.22 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

The Federal Reserve raised rates in September. September consumer prices (CPI) are expected up 0.6 percent on the month and 3.6 percent on the year, with core prices 0.2 percent and 2.5 percent (CNBC, investingLive consensus, Speculation).

Forward (months–years)

CPI today, Wednesday 14 October, at 14:30 Paris, after filing. Fed meeting Tuesday 27 and Wednesday 28 October.

Apple
Fact

Apple launches HomeView display ($299), HomePod mini 2 ($149), new Apple TV 4K ($299). HomeView not for the EU

What happened

At its 'Welcome home' event on Tuesday 13 October Apple announced HomeView, its first smart-home hub, with a 7-inch square screen and A18 chip, in tabletop and wall-mounted versions, both 299 dollars, pre-orders open, available Friday 13 November in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and India (Apple Newsroom). HomeView is 'not available for sale in the European Union, as Siri AI is currently unavailable in iOS in the EU'. HomePod mini 2, with an S10 chip, costs 149 dollars and arrives Friday 30 October, with Siri AI coming in a later software update. A fourth-generation Apple TV 4K costs 299 dollars and ships 13 November, and an Apple Home Collection of sensors and cameras from 39.95 to 399.95 dollars starts arriving in December (Apple Newsroom, Mac Observer, Engadget).

Context (what it sits on)

It is Apple's first new home product category since the HomePod, against Amazon's Echo Show and Google's Nest Hub, and a showcase for its rebuilt Siri. The EU exclusion is a visible cost of Apple's dispute with Brussels over the Digital Markets Act.

Forward (months–years)

HomePod mini 2 on sale Friday 30 October. HomeView and Apple TV Friday 13 November. Apple results late October. Macs and iPad mini reported for later in October (Bloomberg, Claim).

AI
Mixed

Google opens Gemini 4 Argon to everyone, a day after Meta's Muse Wonder

What happened

Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available on Tuesday 13 October in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, its Antigravity coding tool and the Gemini developer interface, with a 1-million-token context window and introductory pricing of 2 dollars per million input tokens and 10 dollars per million output tokens (Google blog). Google pitches it for complex coding, science and agent tasks. It had been limited to vetted cyber defenders since its announcement on 30 September over misuse concerns. Context (what it sits on)

Google is undercutting Meta on price. The staged release of Argon was an unusual safety step for a frontier model.

Forward (months–years)

Watch independent benchmark results, and Alphabet and Meta results (Meta Wednesday 21 October, Alphabet Wednesday 28 October).

Fact

ASML record Q3 sales EUR 11.66bn, Q4 guide EUR 14.2-15.2bn. TSMC Thursday

What happened

ASML, the Dutch maker of the lithography machines used to print advanced chips, reported this morning (Wednesday 14 October, about 07:00 Paris) record third-quarter sales of 11.66 billion euros against guidance of 11.0 to 12.0 billion, a gross margin of 57.5 percent and net income of 4.96 billion euros. ASML no longer publishes quarterly bookings. Investor call at 15:00 Paris today.

Context (what it sits on)

ASML sits at the top of the AI supply chain: its orders show how fast chipmakers and memory makers are adding capacity for AI chips and high-bandwidth memory (Speculation as a read-through). Chip shares have fallen five days running.

Forward (months–years)

TSMC results Thursday 15 October at 08:00 Paris. Microsoft and Alphabet Wednesday 28 October, AMD Tuesday 3 November.

Ethiopia
Mixed

Federal troops reach Zalambessa on the Eritrean border. UN Security Council discusses tensions

What happened

Ethiopian federal forces reached the border town of Zalambessa after advancing through Tigray, stoking fears of interstate war (Reuters via StreetInsider, Critical Threats). The UN Security Council held closed consultations on Ethiopia-Eritrea tensions at US request. Eritrea denies any incursion and asked the Council for measures against Ethiopia. A pro-government Tigray militia claims Eritrean troops have suffered heavy losses, which Eritrea dismisses (Al Arabiya, Claim).

Context (what it sits on)

The two countries fought a border war in 1998 to 2000 and have cut diplomatic ties. Ethiopia wants access to the Red Sea.

Forward (months–years)

Watch any cross-border fighting and African Union mediation.

Cargo flights keep the station supplied while SpaceX pushes Starship and its Starlink Mobile carrier plan.
