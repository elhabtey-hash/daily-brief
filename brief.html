Nvidia (lead)
Mixed

Huang resigns; Kress interim chief, Jones chair

What happened

Nvidia’s press release and a Form 8-K filing on 12 November say the board on 11 November accepted the resignation of Jensen Huang as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately. Colette Kress, 59, chief financial officer since 2013, is interim chief executive and keeps the finance role while the board searches for a permanent successor among internal and external candidates. Harvey Jones, 73, a director since 1993 and former Synopsys chief executive, becomes the company’s first-ever board chair. Jones said operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap should “continue without interruption”. The filing gives no reason for the resignation.

Context (what it sits on)

The announcement came at 14:48 CET on Thursday. Shares fell as much as 14 percent, then closed down 4.34 percent at $166.19, about 30 percent below their 6 October record; Reuters says they trade at about 12 times expected earnings, the lowest valuation since 2012. Context is from press reports, not the company: the Wall Street Journal had reported the board was preparing to remove Huang, and news outlets have reported extramarital relationships and an investigation into whether he was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation; these are claims. The board’s outside investigation, announced 10 November, is not mentioned in the filing. House committee chairs sent second letters and say Huang has agreed in principle to brief them (claim); no date is confirmed and no subpoena has been issued. Trump publicly suggested former Apple chief Tim Cook as successor; that is an opinion, not a process.

Forward (months–years)

Tue 17 Nov ~22:20 CET: third-quarter results, reconfirmed in the release, call 23:00 CET, the first under Kress; Nvidia guided to $108 billion revenue, plus or minus 2 percent. Wed 18 Nov 18:00 CET: staff meeting (claim, from CNBC’s copies of emails). House briefing date not confirmed.

Iran and the Gulf
Mixed

Trump shuts the Vance channel

What happened

Trump wrote on social media that “we don’t need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed,” and said Vice President JD Vance should focus on domestic issues (Washington Post, NBC). This appears to end the possible Vance meeting with Iranian security official Mohsen Rezaei reported this week. Forward (months–years)

No talks, deal or reopening of the strait is assumed. Watch shipping incidents, any US strike, and whether Iran comes up at next week’s Asia-Pacific summit.

Mixed

Science and technology
Fact

How Ebola spread before anyone noticed

What happened

A study in Science, reported by Nature on 12 November, used anonymised mobile phone data and test records to reconstruct the first weeks of the Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The first confirmed patient had symptoms from 25 April, three weeks before the outbreak was declared on 15 May, and movement out of Mongbwalu, Bunia and Rwampara predicted where cases appeared.

Context (what it sits on)

Confirmed infections have passed 10,000, with 5,198 deaths, making it the largest Bundibugyo outbreak recorded; there is no licensed vaccine or approved treatment for this strain. The authors blame surveillance failures, testing gaps and funding cuts. Risk rose again 150–200 kilometres away along travel routes to larger towns.

Forward (months–years)

Watch World Health Organization updates and vaccine trial news.
