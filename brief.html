Palantir
Fact

Thiel bought about 122 million dollars of shares; executive sales were mostly tax bills, not discretionary selling

What happened

Seven Form 4 filings landed late on 24 Nov and change the picture of last week's insider 'selling cluster'. None of the 20 to 24 Nov sales was a discretionary, unplanned sale (Fact, SEC filings read by the Palantir tracker). About 555,852 shares, worth about $85.35 million, were automatic 'sell-to-cover' sales to pay the tax due when restricted stock units vested on 20 Nov. They are coded S, which means an open-market sale, not F, which would mean the company withheld shares to pay the tax, but the footnotes say they were tax-withholding sales under pre-scheduled 10b5-1 trading plans. A further about $13.40 million were open-market sales already scheduled under such plans (chief executive Alex Karp 90,000 shares and chief accounting officer Jeffrey Buckley 1,250). Director Peter Thiel bought 850,100 shares on 20 Nov, about $122.25 million, at an average $143.81, coded P, meaning an open-market purchase, under a 10b5-1 plan adopted on 19 Aug. That purchase is larger than all the executive sales over 20 to 24 Nov (about $98.7 million). Palantir closed Tuesday at $161.18, up 1.14 percent.

Context (what it sits on)

A Form 4 is the legally required report of an insider trade, due within two business days. A 10b5-1 plan is a pre-set trading schedule, adopted when the insider is not supposed to be using inside information. Karp's earlier Form 144 had flagged up to 585,000 shares; 403,263 have been sold so far as tax-cover, so further tax-cover filings are possible. Chief technology officer Shyam Sankar's separate 19 Nov sale of 300,000 shares for about $47.96 million, already logged, was also under a 10b5-1 plan. A secondary report that Thiel's plan allows purchases of up to 4 million shares is unverified and is not in the footnotes read.

Forward (months–years)

Watch for any further Karp tax-cover Form 4s, and for NHS Federated Data Platform procurement terms in the coming weeks. A possible Senate homeland security committee session in early December may or may not include Palantir's Michael LoSapio, whose nomination remains stalled. The Nasdaq-100 reconstitution is expected to be announced on Fri 11 Dec, effective around Mon 21 Dec. Links: US, Markets.

Nvidia
Mixed

Commerce blacklists the alleged diversion network; Bessent's informal deadline is today, still with no public answers

What happened

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) added seven companies to its Entity List, effective 23 Nov and scheduled for publication in the Federal Register today: Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd. (with aliases including Megaspeed and 7Road) and Swiftdata and Apex in Singapore, MSFusion, PT Megaspeed International and Speedmatrix in Malaysia, and Megaspeed (Thailand) (Fact, BIS final rule 2026-23999). The reason given is 'the significant risk of these entities being or becoming involved in the diversion of advanced computing commodities to unauthorized end uses or end users.' Licences are required for all items subject to US export rules and will be reviewed under a 'presumption of denial', meaning they are expected to be refused. This is a Commerce action, not an Nvidia disclosure. Nvidia closed Tuesday at $155.38, down 5.86 percent on about 384 million shares, giving back part of Monday's 31.48 percent jump but still up 23.8 percent over two days from Friday's $125.53 (Yahoo Finance).

Context (what it sits on)

The Entity List is a US blacklist. Being added does not, by itself, prove wrongdoing in court, but it generally stops American firms selling the listed companies technology. Bessent has said Nvidia already took Megaspeed off its sales list in August 2025 (Claim). As of 06:51 CET today, no Treasury or Nvidia statement was found that the 'answers' Bessent informally asked for by about 25 Nov have arrived; this is not a legal deadline, and US official hours are still ahead (Claim as to his request). The independent board investigation continues, and the search for a Chief Compliance Officer has begun (Fact, 23 Nov company filing). California Attorney General Rob Bonta's investigative subpoena of 19 Nov is still not addressed in an 8-K. The reported SEC accounting inquiry remains unconfirmed. Huang resigned per the 8-K of 12 Nov, effective 11 November, and was reinstated per the 8-K of 23 Nov, effective 22 Nov; Harvey Jones remains chair and Colette Kress is chief financial officer. House chairs have reconfirmed Huang's closed-door interview on Tue 1 Dec and general counsel Tim Teter's on Wed 2 Dec (Fact). A Senate Banking public hearing on Thu 3 Dec at 16:00 CET is reported by CNBC, The Hill and TechCrunch, but no official notice was opened, so the date remains a claim.

Forward (months–years)

Watch whether any Bessent or Treasury statement arrives during US hours today, the still-unfiled quarterly 10-Q (due by about Fri 4 Dec), and GTC Washington, which runs Mon 30 Nov to Thu 3 Dec. CNBC says Ian Buck is filling the Tue 1 Dec keynote slot at 20:00 CET and that Nvidia declined to say whether Huang will appear (Claim). Links: Markets, AI companies, US.

AI companies
Mixed

Dell more than doubles revenue and raises its forecast; Anthropic closes below its offer price

What happened

Dell reported fiscal third-quarter results after the US close on Tuesday: record revenue of $54.9 billion, up 103 percent from a year earlier, and adjusted earnings of $9.45 a share, against forecasts of about $49.3 billion and $6.59 (Dell IR; CNBC, Reuters). Revenue from servers designed for artificial intelligence was $22.0 billion, up 291 percent, and the company said its AI order backlog ended the quarter at a record $132.3 billion after $60.2 billion of new orders. It raised its full-year revenue forecast to $202 billion from $192 billion, the third increase this year, and the board approved an extra $25 billion of share buybacks. In regular trading, Dell had closed at $295.28, down 11.83 percent from Monday's close, then rose about 4 percent after hours to around $308 (Investing.com); after-hours prices are not a close. Anthropic (ANT) closed at $81.91, down 6.53 percent, its first close below the $83 offer price (SpaceX tracker; Yahoo).

Context (what it sits on)

Dell's share-price swing shows investors are still arguing about how profitable the AI build-out will be, even when the orders are large. Secondary reports tie Anthropic's break below the offer price to a broad sell-off, early exercise of the extra IPO shares (removing a source of buying support), and insider sales (Axios, Reuters; Claim as to the causes). Hewlett Packard Enterprise on Monday beat forecasts but fell after hours; that after-hours move was not a close.

Forward (months–years)

Watch whether Dell's bounce holds when the US market reopens after Thanksgiving, and Amazon's re:Invent conference in Las Vegas from Mon 30 Nov to Fri 4 Dec, where OpenAI's Sam Altman is listed as a guest at the chief executive keynote on Tue 1 Dec. Links: Markets, Nvidia, Keynotes, SpaceX.

Fed
Fact

Inflation and growth figures due at 14:30 CET; a December rise is almost fully priced

What happened

The Commerce Department is due to publish October personal income and spending, including the personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price index, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, at 14:30 CET today, together with the second estimate of third-quarter growth (BEA schedule). Those figures had not been released when this brief was written and are not anticipated here. CME FedWatch, which turns futures prices into odds, showed on Tuesday evening a 97.4 percent chance of a quarter-point rise at the 8 to 9 Dec meeting and a 2.6 percent chance of a half-point (CoinNess). CNBC on Wednesday morning said traders saw about a 97 percent chance of a rise.

Context (what it sits on)

The Fed raised its rate in September and held it at 3.75 to 4.00 percent in October, so a December rise would be the second of this cycle (Reuters). New York Fed President John Williams said on Monday that inflation is 'too high' and that he would not rule out rises at consecutive meetings. These are the last major official figures before the Fed's pre-meeting blackout on public comment.

Forward (months–years)

Watch the 14:30 CET release, then the Fed meeting on Tue 8 and Wed 9 Dec. A hotter inflation reading would cement a December rise; a cooler one could still leave it largely priced. Links: Markets, Energy.

Morocco
Fact

Sports court restores Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup winner; Morocco says it will keep appealing

What happened

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) in Lausanne ruled on Tue 24 Nov that it upheld the Senegalese Football Federation's appeal, set aside a Confederation of African Football (CAF) appeals-board decision, and reinstated the CAF disciplinary board's result: Senegal won the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final 1-0 (Fact, CAS media release). The referee's decision to restart the match after Senegal had briefly left the pitch was, the panel said unanimously, a 'field of play' matter that sports courts do not second-guess except in cases of bad faith or arbitrariness, which it did not find. CAF said it took note of the award. The Royal Moroccan Football Federation said it would pursue all remaining legal avenues (FRMF via APA).

Context (what it sits on)

That outcome is the opposite of a Spanish newspaper report circulated on Tuesday morning, before the ruling, which said the appeal had been rejected; that report was a claim and was wrong. A further appeal on narrow procedural grounds to the Swiss Federal Tribunal can still be possible. The final itself, in Rabat, had been delayed after Senegal walked off in protest at a late penalty.

Forward (months–years)

Watch whether Morocco files at the Swiss Federal Tribunal, and any CAF or Moroccan government comment. Links: Longer term (Morocco).

Russia-Ukraine war
Fact

Strike on Zaporizhzhia kills two and wounds dozens, including children

What happened

A Russian night-time attack on Zaporizhzhia, in southern Ukraine, killed two civilians and wounded 48 people, including four children, officials said on Tuesday (AP). Ukrainian sources said a private house and part of a five-storey building were destroyed. The Kyiv Independent, citing officials, later put the nationwide toll for the day at nine killed and 81 injured. President Zelensky said on Monday that intelligence showed Russia preparing new strikes and urged the United States to supply more Patriot interceptors.

Context (what it sits on)

This follows Sunday's apartment-block strike in Dymer, north of Kyiv, which killed at least 11 people, and Zelensky's confirmation that Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury in Libya. At the UN, Russia has ruled out any new Black Sea grain deal.

Forward (months–years)

Europe
Fact

Leaders meet tomorrow on a 45 billion euro Ukraine loan, with four countries already opting out

What happened

EU leaders gather in Brussels on Thu 26 and Fri 27 Nov to discuss a proposed 45 billion euro loan to Ukraine for 2027, backed by joint borrowing, and the next long-term EU budget. Slovakia's prime minister, Robert Fico, has said Slovakia will not take part, as it declined to join an earlier loan; Hungary, the Czech Republic and Bulgaria have also indicated they will stay out (Kyiv Post, EUalive, Euronews). The Commission's 22nd sanctions package, presented on Monday, still needs unanimity.

Context (what it sits on)

Support for Ukraine is one of the few points of agreement in a wider fight over the size of the next EU budget, but governments disagree on whether the loan should sit inside the common budget and how the burden should be shared. Poland's Donald Tusk has also expressed reservations about joint borrowing for this purpose (Euronews).

Forward (months–years)

Watch how many members opt out, and whether the sanctions package, including the SWIFT ban on about 60 Russian banks, is agreed. Links: Russia-Ukraine war, NATO eastern flank.
