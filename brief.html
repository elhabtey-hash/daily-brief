Tesla
Mixed

Tesla beats on profit but margins stay slim

What happened

Tesla reported third-quarter revenue of 28.05 billion dollars and adjusted earnings of 48 cents a share, ahead of forecasts (8-K and shareholder deck, 21 Oct, CNBC/LSEG). Profit under standard accounting (GAAP) was 787 million dollars, or 22 cents a share, down from 1.37 billion a year earlier. Gross margin was 19.6 percent and operating margin 2.6 percent, as operating costs rose 39 percent with research spending of 2.76 billion. Free cash flow was 1.53 billion dollars and cash reached a record 45.4 billion. Energy storage deployments were 13.7 gigawatt-hours. Paid Full Self-Driving (FSD) subscriptions reached 1.74 million. Shares fell about 2 to 3 percent after hours.

Context (what it sits on)

Investors are paying for the future rather than today's car profits. Management said demand exceeds production by a significant margin, limited by battery supply, and that Megapack storage is almost sold out for 2027. Musk said Europe-wide approval of FSD could come within a few months and that he would be surprised if it came later than the first quarter of 2027 (claim). The EU vehicle committee has not voted on it. He said robotaxis would become financially noticeable in the second quarter of 2027, with a Dallas Cybercab launch next month. The finance chief said spending will keep rising, including on AI computing and a chip plant, backed by up to 30 billion dollars of borrowing capacity. The call added no substantive detail on the board committees studying a possible SpaceX combination, which is not agreed.

Forward (months–years)

Cybercab answers to the US road-safety regulator (NHTSA) are due Friday 30 October. China incentives end Saturday 31 October. The 10-Q filing comes late October or early November. Annual meeting Thursday 3 December, 22:00 Paris.

AI companies
Fact

Meta beats and raises its spending floor

What happened

Meta reported third-quarter revenue of 64.60 billion dollars, up 26 percent and above the 63.37 billion expected (Meta investor release, CNBC/LSEG). Capital spending was 34.91 billion dollars in the quarter. It narrowed its 2026 capital spending range to 135 to 145 billion dollars from 130 to 145 billion, and guided fourth-quarter revenue to 73 to 76 billion. A 9.89 billion dollar legal charge pushed free cash flow negative, to minus 2.12 billion. Shares rose about 2 to 3 percent after hours. Netflix fell more than 5 percent on Wednesday after cutting its advertising outlook.

Context (what it sits on)

Meta gave no 2027 spending number, saying planning is highly dynamic. Higher spending supports chip suppliers such as Nvidia but shows how much AI build-out now costs even the most profitable firms. Negative free cash flow keeps the question of who pays for AI alive. Overnight Nasdaq-100 futures fell about 1.3 percent, so the market reaction is not settled.

Forward (months–years)

Microsoft and Alphabet report Wednesday 28 October, Amazon about Thursday 29 October.

SpaceX
Fact

First big lockup ends today and the Starlink launch slips again

What happened

Up to 1,313.6 million SpaceX shares become eligible for sale today, Thursday 22 October, under the listing prospectus terms (lockup trackers, no new filing). A further 328.4 million are freed on Saturday 24 October and tradable from Monday 26 October. The Starlink 15-15 mission was scrubbed for the second night on Wednesday after a weather hold and is now targeted for 00:40 Paris on Friday 23 October. Falcon launches this year stay at 119. SpaceX shares rose about 7 to 8 percent on Wednesday after its results, and several banks raised price targets (CNBC).

Context (what it sits on)

Eligibility is not the same as selling. The only insider sale on file so far is by president Gwynne Shotwell in September. The strong results may absorb supply, but lockup weeks often bring volatility. Musk's remark about 500 billion to 1 trillion dollars of annual recurring revenue by end-2027 is aspiration, not guidance.

Forward (months–years)

Starlink 15-16 on Saturday 24 October, 07:29 to 11:29 Paris. US telecoms regulator (FCC) open meeting on satellite-to-phone spectrum Thursday 29 October. Starship Flight 15 around Sunday 8 November is a press date. 10-Q by about Monday 9 November.

Global markets

Wall Street extends gains, futures slip after results

Fed decision Wednesday 28 October. Bank of Korea next meets Thursday 26 November.

Mixed

Markets expect a pause next week but a December rise

What happened

The 10-year Treasury yield was about 5.12 percent on Wednesday, down about 3 basis points (Federal Reserve H.15, press). CME FedWatch priced about an 84 to 85 percent chance that the Federal Reserve holds rates at its meeting ending Wednesday 28 October and about 15 to 16 percent for a quarter-point rise. Forward (months–years)

Iran and Hormuz
Mixed

Talks continue through mediators but Iran rejects US demands

What happened

Iran's top security official Mohsen Rezaei told Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir, in Tehran this week, that Iran rejects the American demands and will not let enriched nuclear material leave the country (IRNA, Al Hadath via Newsquawk). Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said indirect talks via Pakistan and Qatar continue without interruption, and that Washington accepted Iran's seven-day plan but wants to add clauses (Arab News). Pakistan's foreign ministry confirmed talks on Thursday. Shipping attacks continued, with at least two more vessels reported struck overnight Tuesday into Wednesday. The US issued new Iran sanctions while allowing some blocked ships to unload non-Iranian cargo.

Context (what it sits on)

The New Arab reports a Qatar-Pakistan middle-ground plan: a secret, unannounced nuclear inspection in return for access to interest on 6 billion dollars of frozen Iranian funds in Qatar (claim, anonymous sources). The core gap is order of steps: Washington wants the nuclear issue first, Tehran wants sanctions relief and the strait reopened first.

Forward (months–years)

Signal is any formal US reply to the plan or a rise in ship crossings.

Energy
Fact

Oil ends flat as US stocks fall and diplomacy hopes linger

What happened

December Brent settled at 108.82 dollars, up 0.03 from Tuesday's 108.79. December WTI settled at 97.15 dollars, down 0.67 from 97.82, in its first session as the front month (Reuters, WSJ, Xinhua). Both had been up more than 2 dollars earlier. The US Energy Information Administration (EIA) reported a 3.1 million barrel fall in crude stocks, larger than expected, while distillate stocks rose 1.7 million barrels. US output fell by 410,000 barrels a day to 13.27 million.

Context (what it sits on)

December WTI is the front month. The gap between Brent and WTI of about 11.7 dollars reflects risk priced into seaborne oil. Saudi Aramco's Yanbu and Shaybah sites were reported undamaged after Houthi drone claims (Argus).

Forward (months–years)

December Brent expires Friday 30 October.

Ukraine
Mixed

Russia agrees in principle to new three-way talks

What happened

After Steve Witkoff met Vladimir Putin in St Petersburg on Wednesday, Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia is ready in principle to resume technical three-way talks with the United States and Ukraine around 10 November, possibly in Abu Dhabi, focused on a post-war security system (AP, Axios, BBC). Ukraine accepted the format in Oslo on Tuesday. Russian missiles hit Kyiv on Wednesday night, injuring six. Zelensky told Fox News he could accept a freeze along the current front line.

Context (what it sits on)

Three-way talks have been paused since February. Ushakov called the Oslo talks not very useful, so the gap on substance is wide. Trump said a settlement is the closest it has been, which is his own assessment.

Forward (months–years)

Proposed talks around Tuesday 10 November.

Nvidia
Fact

Nvidia expands supply chain and wins a big Google Cloud commitment

What happened

At its GTC Berlin keynote on Wednesday Nvidia said partners will expand chip substrate capacity 2.5 times over three years, Amkor will raise packaging capacity by more than 300 percent by end-2028, and Nvidia will advance 1.1 billion dollars to KYEC for US testing capacity (Nvidia press release). Google Cloud said its A5X instances on Nvidia's Vera Rubin systems will be generally available next month and that it plans up to 960,000 Rubin chips. Nvidia also showed DSX POD, a containerised AI data centre deployable in 14 days.

Context (what it sits on)

The supply moves address the bottlenecks in packaging and substrates that limit how many chips Nvidia can ship. The Google commitment is a counterpoint to worries that Google's own TPU chips will replace Nvidia's.

Forward (months–years)

GTC Berlin ends today. DGX Spark 64GB goes on sale Friday 23 October. Nvidia third-quarter results around Tuesday 17 November (claim until confirmed).

Palantir
Fact

Marines open Maven to all units ahead of results

What happened

The US Marine Corps signed an enterprise licence on 1 October making Palantir's Maven Smart System available to all commands, including reserves, on any authorised network (Military Times, 21 Oct). Before, only specific commands had access, on the secret network only. Palantir also announced customers fine-tuning their own AI models, including Nvidia's Nemotron, inside its software.

Context (what it sits on)

Maven is becoming a default across US forces. Palantir's Europe chief said European rivals are not battle-tested, as some European buyers push for local suppliers. No dollar values were disclosed.

Forward (months–years)

Seoul event 22 to 23 October. Third-quarter results Monday 2 November, with guidance of 2.160 to 2.164 billion dollars of revenue against Street forecasts near 2.18 billion.
