Israel
Mixed

No clear winner in the election

What happened

Polls closed at 21:00 Paris time on Tuesday 27 October with turnout of 77.7 percent at closing, the highest at that hour since 1999 (Central Elections Committee via BBC). Exit polls from Kan and Channel 13 give Netanyahu's bloc and the opposition bloc led by former army chief Gadi Eisenkot 51 seats each, and Channel 12 gives 52 to 49, with the two Arab parties at a record 18 to 19 (Reuters, BBC). With 55.4 percent of ballot boxes counted at 07:43 Israel time, the partial official count gives Netanyahu's bloc 56 seats and the opposition 49 (Central Elections Committee via Times of Israel). Netanyahu claimed a 'great majority' for his bloc. About 600,000 absentee ballots, mostly soldiers', are counted only by Thursday.

Context (what it sits on)

A majority needs 61 of 120 seats, and neither bloc has it on any measure so far. Coalition talks could take weeks and a repeat election is possible. The outcome shapes Israel's conduct of the Iran war and relations with Washington. Partial counts often shift as soldiers' votes come in, so Netanyahu's claim is a claim.

Forward (months–years)

Absentee count by Thursday 29 October, official results and President's consultations next week.

AI companies
Mixed

Oracle puts Nvidia's Vera Rubin on sale, SK hynix posts record profit

What happened

Nvidia and Oracle said on Tuesday 27 October that Vera Rubin NVL72 systems and Vera processors are now generally available on Oracle Cloud and that Oracle plans to expand them to multi-gigawatt scale (Nvidia release). OpenAI is an early user of Vera processors there. SK hynix, the main supplier of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI chips, reported record third-quarter revenue of 104.6 trillion won and an operating profit of 83.3 trillion won, an 80 percent margin (SK hynix release). It said HBM demand exceeds supply by more than two times and that 2027 volumes and prices are agreed with major customers (earnings call, claim). AMD said it will put 1 billion dollars into Meta's share sale alongside Nvidia's 3 billion (CNBC, claim).

Context (what it sits on)

This is evidence that the next generation of AI hardware is shipping on time and that memory remains the tight spot. It eased last week's fear about AI spending, but it also means chip customers will pay more for memory in 2027. Nvidia and AMD investing in Meta, which then buys their chips, adds to concerns about circular financing.

Forward (months–years)

Microsoft and Alphabet tonight, Samsung, Amazon and Intel Thursday 29 October, AMD Tuesday 3 November.

Iran and the Gulf
Mixed

Houthis target Ras Tanura as Iran restates its terms

What happened

Yemen's Houthis said on Tuesday 27 October that a ballistic missile hit Saudi Aramco's Ras Tanura refinery and export terminal on the Gulf coast (Reuters, claim). Saudi authorities said they intercepted three missiles aimed at the Eastern Province and two at Riyadh, and that a fire near Ras Tanura was contained, with damage unclear (AP, Xinhua). Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei said the war can end only if conditions set by Iranian officials are met, which reportedly include ending the US naval blockade and lifting sanctions (Straits Times). President Pezeshkian met Vladimir Putin in Moscow, where Putin said Russia keeps its commitments on Iranian projects including the Bushehr nuclear plant (Kremlin).

Context (what it sits on)

Ras Tanura is one of the world's largest oil export terminals, so any real damage would matter more than most Houthi strikes. Iran is signalling openness to ending the war on its terms while seeking Russian backing. Talks through Pakistan, Qatar and Saudi Arabia continue but there is no ceasefire.

Forward (months–years)

Damage assessment at Ras Tanura, US midterms Tuesday 3 November.

Energy
Fact

Oil falls a second day on diplomacy

What happened

December Brent settled at 113.88 dollars on Tuesday 27 October, down 1.93 from 115.81, and December WTI at 99.90 dollars, down 1.86 from 101.76 (ICE and NYMEX via Xinhua, Reuters, CNBC). The January Brent contract settled at 109.41 dollars (Newsquawk). Prices briefly rose on the Ras Tanura report, then fell when Khamenei's comments were read as openness to ending the war. Gold rose 0.9 percent to about 4,075 dollars an ounce.

Context (what it sits on)

December WTI is the front month and has dipped below 100 dollars. December Brent expires on Friday 30 October, after which the January contract, about 4.5 dollars cheaper, becomes the headline price, so Brent will appear to drop even if nothing changes. UBS expects Brent near 110 dollars to the end of the year.

Forward (months–years)

December Brent expiry Friday 30 October.

Tesla
Mixed

Texas robotaxi fleet jumps as Cybercab answers fall due

What happened

The Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV) register shows 573 Cybercabs authorised, up 110 from the previous morning, and 507 Model Ys, 1,080 robotaxis in total (tracker data pull). Registrations are not active rides, and Cybercab service runs in Austin only. Tesla widened its Houston service area to much of the 610 Loop on Tuesday (Axios). Tesla says Optimus robot production has started at Fremont, with customer deliveries likely late 2027 (earnings call, claim). Tesla's sworn answers to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) on Cybercab safety certification, audit AQ26002, are due Friday 30 October.

Context (what it sits on)

The fleet now matches the roughly 1,000 vehicles NHTSA assumed in its audit, so regulators' view of a car with no steering wheel is the main hurdle. A Dallas Cybercab launch in November is a company claim. Any Tesla and SpaceX combination is only under study by a SpaceX board committee and nothing is agreed.

Forward (months–years)

NHTSA answers Friday 30 October, China incentives end Saturday 31 October, China October wholesale about 2 to 4 November.

SpaceX
Fact

Starlink 15-16 flies, spectrum vote Thursday

What happened

Falcon 9 launched Starlink 15-16 from Vandenberg at 08:25 Paris time on Tuesday 27 October with 27 satellites, and the booster landed at sea on its 26th flight (SpaceX). That is the 121st Falcon launch this year. The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) votes on Thursday 29 October on satellite-to-phone spectrum items, and SpaceX has asked it to add a 902 to 928 MHz band (Communications Daily). No new company filing since the 26 October lease filing, and the Apollo-led GPU lease is committed but not closed.

Context (what it sits on)

More spectrum for direct-to-phone service would let Starlink compete more directly with mobile networks. The launch pace keeps building towards Starship Flight 15, which the press dates to about 8 November without a licence yet.

Forward (months–years)

FCC Thursday 29 October, Starlink 15-18 Friday 30 October 05:02 Paris, USSF-432 Monday 2 November.

Ukraine
Mixed

Oleshky truce holds as Moscow talks of escalation

What happened

The US-brokered local ceasefire around Russian-occupied Oleshky in the Kherson region took effect on Tuesday 27 October, allowing aid in and evacuations, and both sides say it will last until late Sunday 1 November (AP, Anadolu). Russia's foreign minister Sergei Lavrov said the war is likely to continue and escalate, and Moscow has not accepted a general ceasefire (Newsquawk, Ukrainian press). Two European leaders had what the Guardian called a frank call with Putin.

Context (what it sits on)

It is a small humanitarian step, but a test of whether the two sides can keep a deal before proposed three-way talks in November.

Forward (months–years)

Truce end Sunday 1 November, three-way talks about 10 November (claim).

France
Fact

Deputies scrap the big-company surtax

What happened

During the 2027 budget debate on Tuesday 27 October, the National Assembly rejected the government's extension of the surtax on large companies by 79 votes to 77, against the government's wishes (Le Figaro). The vote on the revenue side is set for Tuesday 3 November and on the whole budget for Tuesday 17 November (franceinfo).

Context (what it sits on)

Losing the surtax widens the hole the government must fill to meet its deficit target, and shows how weak its control of the Assembly is. The Senate may restore it.

Forward (months–years)

Revenue vote Tuesday 3 November.

Morocco
Claim

UN Western Sahara vote set for Friday

What happened

The United States circulated a revised draft resolution on 26 October that would make Morocco's autonomy plan the sole basis for negotiations, while reportedly keeping the UN mission MINURSO's mandate to 31 October 2027 (Morocco World News, Le360). The Security Council vote is expected on Friday 30 or Saturday 31 October, and Russia's position is seen as decisive (claim).

Context (what it sits on)

Adoption would be another diplomatic win for Rabat over the Polisario Front and Algeria. A Russian veto or abstention would shape how strong the text is.

Forward (months–years)

Security Council vote about 30 to 31 October.
